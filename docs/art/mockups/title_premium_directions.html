Recommendation

C+, built in an order that keeps A+ as the fallback. C+ is the only direction that looks like an authored title scene rather than the game dimmed behind a menu, which is the premium gap in the current title. A storefront is a place you arrive at, the way Stardew's farm sky or Celeste's mountain are.

Build order: first the shared pieces (the logo, the till digit font, the menu_plate frames and the tick marker, toy_machine_bare and its aisle derives, the REWARDS screen), then the storefront scene (storefront_night.txt, 640x360). If the facade art stalls, the shared pieces drop onto the A+ interior with no work wasted.

Toy machines and Loyalty share one entry, REWARDS. Both are store rewards paid in coins, so the coin count is shown once, where coins are spent. The top level stays at three entries (like Balatro's Collection or Slay the Spire's Compendium), and Loyalty arrives later without a new title button. In C+ the glass bay is the same entry drawn as a place, not a second button. Other names to offer: BACK ROOM, PRIZE COUNTER.
