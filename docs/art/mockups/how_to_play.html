How to Play: the quick card at the register

Your request: "A small How to Play entry in the pause menu could cover controls and terms that are easy to forget mid-run, such as CHAIN, MORNING, and when a basket redraw is available." This page shows the entry in the pause menu (the same four entries as the options/pause mock-up) and three versions of a one-screen card. The card is the short version. Each word on it opens its page in the Staff Handbook (PR #143), which stays the full reference.

The stage is the game's 1920x1080 frame over a real capture (shift 2, Training week, fb-p3). Click inside it: the menu button (top right) or Esc opens the pause menu, and every word with a › opens its handbook page. All rule text is taken from data/handbook/entries/*.tres on feature/keywords-handbook, data/balance/balance.tres and the plan.

The three directions

A · Laminated cardRECOMMENDED

A laminated cheat card clipped to the register. It slides up from the register with a sheen, tilted like a real card. One screen, three columns: Hands (controls), Words on the belt (CHAIN, BATCH, PAIR, MORNING, WASTED, GOLDEN) and The shift (redraw, target and laps, the machine). Words on today's belt wear a gold "ON YOUR BELT" corner.

Everything fits on one screen, so there are no tabs to learn. It reads as an object in the store, like the time card and the day slip.

B · Tabbed card

A monitor-style card with four tabs (Controls · Words · Redraw · Target & machine; keys 1 to 4). Each tab has room for bigger text and a small live example.

The text is larger and each tab gets its own example. The cost is that the answer can be one tab away, and a quick reference shouldn't make the player hunt.

C · Today first

A clipboard from the right that reads today's state first: the words on your belt and in your baskets, with a hint for each ("Cheese needs Bread beside it"), the redraws left and today's event. Hovering a line lights the item on the stage. Controls and the other words are folded below.

It's the most helpful card, but it's a new rules-explainer that has to stay correct for every card and event. It also overlaps the planning effects and the monitor.

What the game does today

1 2 3 4
1
No pause. The ☰ menu holds Export log, and on PR #143 also Guide (the handbook). Esc does nothing on the shift screen. Planning time pauses only when the window loses focus (FocusWatch).
2
The redraw rule isn't written anywhere you can see it. The register shows "2 LEFT" and REDRAW. When a redraw works (before CHECKOUT, on a basket that still holds an item, while items are left to draw) is only in the handbook's Redraw entry, which says only "2 per shift".
3
Words are explained one item at a time. The scan popup and tips explain the item under the pointer. Their right-click opens the handbook ("Right-click: open the guide"). Nothing gathers the controls in one place.
4
The handbook is complete but deep. It has 37 entries in 6 tabs (Keywords, Families, Coupons, Tiers, Shift, Places). It's a good reference, but too much for "what was MORNING again?". Laps, overtime tickets and the machine have no entry.

Recommendation: A, with C's "on your belt" marks

Build A. A quick reference should answer at a glance, and A puts every answer on one screen. It also looks like the store's other paper (the time card and the day slip) instead of a menu. Take one idea from C: a gold ON YOUR BELT corner on the words that today's row and hand use. It is cheap because it reads the same keyword marks the belt already draws (KeywordMarks, PR #143), and it points the player to the right line first without a second rules engine.

B is the fallback if A's text is too small at 1280x720 or with the larger text-size setting. Its tabs can hold the same rows at a larger size, so the content data would be the same.
