One line for the run's best moment

Your idea: the final receipt prints one line that sums up the run's best moment, like Biggest payout · Golden Milk · 264€, so a player leaves with one result to remember or share. This page shows where that line goes on today's receipt, how it prints, which moments can be the highlight and how one is picked, and what the game would have to start recording. Click the stage (or Tap) to do what a tap does on the results screen: print the rest at once.

2 · Three directions

A

One line

RECOMMENDED

Exactly your idea. After the shift history, below the rule, the receipt prints one more line: a gold band, a starburst sticker reading BEST, the item's 7x7 mini, then Biggest payout · Golden Milk · 264€ and the shift on the right. Then the coins print as today.

It's printed when its turn comes, like any other line. The band wipes in (200 ms), the sticker lands with today's stamp sound and a 50 ms hit-stop, a glint crosses the band once, and the print holds 300 ms before the coins. That comes to about 0.65 s more than today.

Cost: one receipt line, one sticker sprite (the planning stickers' burst, recoloured), no new sound.

B

Highlight stub

The receipt prints a short gold line at its foot (★ BEST MOMENT › see stub). After the coins, a gold stub slides out from behind the receipt's left edge and is taped to the shutter: the item at full sprite size, the kind, the name, the amount in big gold digits, and the shift.

It's the most collectable version and the best-looking share image, and it sits on the left so the unlock cards (badge pins) can keep the right side. But it adds a card, a slide and a second place to read, and a "Closest call" or "Over quota" stub has no item to show.

Cost: a stub scene, a sunburst, about 1 s more, and its own layout tests.

C

Pen circle

No new line. When the coins have printed, a red marker circles the shift line where the moment happened, and a scrawled note beside it says what it was (BEST! Golden Milk 264€), like a manager marking up a till roll.

It costs no space (good for the 16-shift receipt that already steps its font down), and it points at when the moment happened. But it isn't a line you can quote, the note has little room in the right column, and on the compact receipt the circle covers its neighbours.

Cost: a drawn ellipse and a note, positioned from the line's rect.

3 · Which moment: the candidates and how one is picked

Each kind finds its best case in the run. Its score is that value divided by a bar (the line where a moment feels special). The bars go in balance data, not in code. The highest score wins. On equal scores the kinds rank in the order of this table. When no kind reaches its bar (score under 1), the line still shows Biggest payout, so every run gets one. Within a kind, the bigger value wins, then the later shift (the bigger build, the fresher memory), then the earlier slot. A Day off shift has no checkout and is skipped. The table follows the run picked above; the gold row is the one the receipt prints.

Considered and left out of the automatic pick: Biggest checkout (quotas climb every shift, so the raw biggest is nearly always one of the last shifts; Over quota is its fair version) and Rarest prize (a LEGENDARY ticket is the machine's luck, not the player's play). Both can still be shown with the HIGHLIGHT menu.

5 · Lost runs, ties and edge cases

  • Lost runs get the line too, picked the same way over every shift played, including the one that ended the run. A loss is when a bright spot helps most. Switch RUN to Lost · shift 11: no payout reached its bar, so Closest call · Shift 9 · 102€ / 101€ wins (a pass by 1€). The line is gold even on a red receipt: it's a good memory, not a verdict.
  • A weak run (Lost · week 1) reaches no bar at all, so it falls back to Biggest payout (Eggs, 12€). That's modest but true, and it shows the player what an item can pay.
  • Ties: equal scores go to the higher kind in the table. Within a kind: the bigger value, then the later shift, then the earlier slot. The same run always prints the same line (no randomness, so a resumed run matches).
  • Copies: Biggest payout names the card (Golden Milk), never "Milk ×3". A SHELF upgrade's copies each pay on their own line, so the best single copy counts.
  • Double checkout (badge pin): both rows count as one shift's checkout. Day off: skipped. Debug jumps (debug_jumped): the line still prints, because the history is real.
  • Fit: in week columns the line spans both columns (like the rules and the coins). On the compact receipt it stays one line and its band shrinks with the font step.

7 · What the game does today

The results screen (#61, #56) closes the store's shutter and prints the final receipt one line every 80 ms: the headline, a rule, each shift's line (total / quota, pass, slip or fail) with dim lines for the machine visits and the day's events, the shortfall on a lost run, a rule, then the coins. A 16-shift run prints in week columns. Then the badge pins it unlocked come one by one on the right. Nothing on the receipt names a single item or moment: the biggest number a player sees is a shift total, and the item payouts from the count-up are gone once the shift ends (only the last checkout's result is kept). Captures below are from the four-weeks build (docs/art/mockups/four_weeks_build/). The coins have lost their "Overtime" line since (v1.03).

Recommended: A · One line, between the history and the coins

  • It's the request as written: one line you can read aloud or share. The format Kind · Subject · Value works for every kind, including the ones without an item.
  • It follows the receipt's own rhythm (one more printed line, a sticker, a short hold) and comes just before the coins, so the run's story ends on its best moment before the reward. It adds about 0.65 s, and a tap still prints everything at once.
  • It's the cheapest to build: one line and a recoloured sticker, no new screen space beyond one line. It leaves the right side free for the badge-pin cards, and it goes into #68's PNG for free.
  • B's stub is a good later step if #68 is built and the share image wants an item at full size. It can reuse A's pick and data unchanged.

8 · Behaviour spec (A)

  • Core: a pure RunHighlight (core/run_highlight.gd) builds the candidates from run.history and picks one by the section 3 rules. Its bars are @export fields on a HighlightDefinition resource in data/balance/ (illustrative values: payout ≥ 0.5 × that shift's quota, over quota ≥ ×2.0, a combo of ≥ 5, a pass by ≤ 1€). Kind order and wording live there too.
  • ShiftRecord gains best_payout, best_card and best_combo, filled in RunState.checkout from the ScoreResult. They're written to the save and the log only when set (additive; no save version bump).
  • FinalReceipt gains Tone.HIGHLIGHT: one line after the second rule, before the coins. It's WIDE_WIDTH in week columns and fits the compact receipt (the FITS search counts it).
  • ResultsPanel prints it in its turn: the band wipes in for 200 ms, then the BEST sticker lands with stamp and a 50 ms hit-stop. The glint takes 450 ms and the hold before the coins 300 ms. The timings are final_highlight_* fields in CountUpTuning. A tap prints it in its final state with the rest. Reduced motion: no wipe, slam or glint, the hold stays. The music's duck and the coin sounds still start with the coin lines.
  • Art: the sticker is sticker_burst_polished recoloured gold with "BEST" (a new @derive source), plus the existing 7x7 minis. A kind without an item (Over quota, Closest call) shows target_euro instead.
  • Telemetry: checkout gains best_payout and best_card, run_end gains highlight. These go into docs/PROTOTYPE_PLAN.md section 8's table, with an optional count in log_summary.py.
  • Tests: core tests for each kind, the bars, the fallback, ties, Day off, Double checkout and an old save without the fields. A UI geometry test checks that the highlight line sits inside the panel and above the buttons on the won 16-shift receipt and on the compact one.
  • Docs: the plan's section 6.2 receipt list and a changelog row (playtest-facing).

Questions (each has a default)

  1. Direction? Default: A, one line between the history and the coins. B's stub can come later with #68.
  2. Which kinds go into the automatic pick? Default: Biggest payout, Over quota, Longest combo (CHAIN / BATCH / MORNING), First GOLDEN, Closest call. Biggest checkout and Rarest prize stay out.
  3. Lost runs? Default: yes, every run gets the line, with the same pick and Biggest payout as the fallback, even after a week-1 loss.
  4. Wording of the label? Default: BEST on the sticker and Kind · Subject · Value as the line (the format from your example). The alternatives are "HIGHLIGHT", "BEST MOMENT" or "RECORD".
