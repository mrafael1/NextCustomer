What each variant does, exactly

K1 one tank: the digits and the € are drawn as one glass layer (pale gold with a glare); a single liquid surface spans the whole group, at the shown total / the quota: empty at the bottom of the ink, full exactly at the top of the tallest glyph. Until the payout that meets the quota lands, it stops at 96 % (so the number never looks full before the win); on that landing it fills over the brim, in the same frame as the win. It follows the count's ticks on a stiff spring (a little slosh, no lag you can see). On each landing the surface sloshes, a ripple spreads both ways from the group's middle, droplets jump off it and bubbles rise.

K2 lockstep: the same level with no spring: it moves exactly one step per tick of the count, a hard flat line with a 2 px bright edge that flashes white on each tick and on each landing. Below the line the digits and € are lit gold bands (FONT C), above it they are unlit grey-beige. Same 96 % hold as K1.

K3 gold bar: the digits and € are FONT C solid gold all the time. A liquid bar (ink outline, gold bands) runs under the digits and the €, from the first digit to the end of the €; its fill is the total / the quota, with a bright lip that flashes on each tick and splashes on each landing. Same 96 % hold. The FONT C shine flashes the whole filled bar at once. The "€N to go" line moves 8 px down, the stamp and the tab 10 px, to make room.

Timing and what stays

The win fires on the landing in K1-K3: the payout that crosses the quota lands, the digits punch, the gold goes from 96 % to full, and the fountain, flash, stamp and sunburst start in that same frame. (The other choice was to fire the win the moment the rolling number reads the quota, mid-roll; I kept the landing so the punch, the fill and the win are one beat.) V1 keeps v1's 0.2 s wait so you can compare.

Kept as in v1 / the game: the bay and its scan look, the number and € size and place (in K1-K3 the size is the one that fits the run's final total, and the € stays put while the digits grow left; V1 re-centres like v1), the "+€N" chip flying into the digits, the "€N to go" line, the SPEND MET stamp and the "+€N" tab, the count holding 0.5 s at the quota. The digits punch about their bottom right corner so they keep the €'s baseline; in K1 and K2 the gold inside the punching digits stays at the same height as in the €, so the waterline never breaks.

Reduced motion: no particles, shakes, punches, ripples, shines or sunburst turn; the level follows the total straight away.
