Start screen: see the whole cart before you open the store

Today the start is a step inside the cart reveal (#107). It shows the staples and the three packs, but today's 6 products sit half hidden as pictures in the dispenser window, and the pins and the stock's aisles aren't shown at all. You see the cart as a whole only after you take a pack. This page redraws that step so that one 1080p screen shows, before you confirm: (1) the three starter packs, (2) the aisles the 6 products are dealt from, (3) the badge pins you wear, with their rules, and (4) the resulting cart as real tickets, grouped and counted. Then one confirm: OPEN STORE ›.

Try it: hover a pack and the cart preview swaps to it live. Click a pack (or press 1, 2 or 3) to pick it. Hover a ticket to read its rule, hover an aisle to see which of today's 6 came from it, and hover a pin for its rule. Use CHANGE PINS to wear a different pin, then Enter or OPEN STORE to confirm. The three profiles are real deals from StartingDeck.deal_offer, run headless on this branch's data (seeds 20261103 and 20261014 on a new profile, seed 99 on a profile with Fruit & veg listed).

1920×1080 frame, scaled to fit. Item art at 2× (96 px tickets) and glyphs at 3×, nearest-neighbour. The backdrop is the game's 1080p shift capture from shift_scene_polish v5, dimmed as the cart reveal dims it.

Variants

A

Clock-in board

RECOMMENDED

One steel board over the store, in four numbered parts read left to right: ① packs on top, ② YOUR CART on a sheet of receipt paper under them (staples · pack · today's deal · pins, each a labelled bracket of tickets with ×N counts and the 13/15 equation), and on the right ③ TODAY'S STOCK (the aisle signs, each marked DEALT FROM, PRIZES ONLY, NOT LISTED or LOCKED) and ④ BADGE PINS (the name badge and its rules). OPEN STORE › is the red register key at the bottom right.

B

Cart receipt

The packs are stacked as three rows on the left, and the cart prints as a long receipt in the centre in the shift's receipt language: mini glyphs, names and ×N, under section headers. It reprints on each hover. Stock and pins sit on the right. It is the most compact to read, but it shows the items as text lines, not as tickets.

C

Shelf wall

The most diegetic option. The packs stand on a top shelf as shrink-wrapped bundles, the aisle signs hang from the ceiling, today's 6 stand on a wooden shelf with price tags, and the staples and the pack's cards fill a basket. The badge hangs on its lanyard. It is charming, but the cart is split across a shelf and a basket, so the 13/15 total reads less clearly.

Why A
  • It's the only layout where the cart reads as one object: one sheet, one 13/15 equation, every card as the same ticket it will be on the belt, grouped by where it came from. That is the user's point (4), the part the current screen leaves until after the pick.
  • The cause and the effect sit side by side: hover a pack and its bracket swaps in the sheet just below. Hover an aisle and its dealt tickets light up. The connection never has to be explained.
  • It keeps the reveal's agreed language: padlocked staples, family-coloured pack faces, GOOD FIRST PICK, 1 to 3 and Enter. So the build is a rework of PackChoice and RevealPanel, not a new screen, and the 2026-10-22 decision of "no pre-run screen" stands: it is still the reveal over shift 1.
  • It fits 1080p with room to spare. Ticket names are 15 px bold, rules are 17 to 20 px, and nothing is under 15 px. At 1280×720 it is the same layout at 2/3 scale, because art sits at whole art px on the 1280 stage.

What the screen must say: findings from the current flow

  • The deal is already made before the pick. StartingDeck.deal_offer draws the three packs and today's 6 together, the 6 are the same whichever pack you take, and the run is saved with them before the choice opens (StartChoice.begin_if_choosing). So showing the whole cart before the confirm needs no earlier deal and no rule change. It is display only. The reveal only hides the 6 today by heaping them in the dispenser window.
  • No re-deal. #107 ruled the reroll out, and the saved offer stops a quit from rerolling it. The seed shows in the header for the record, the same as on the shift's register. Nothing is clickable.
  • Where the 6 come from: the stock's fresh-stock products that have a tier chain. Capsule products are never dealt (random_capsules is off). On a new profile that means the 6 staples plus the 14 base items of Cold cases, Pantry and Household, 20 products in all. A machine-opened aisle is never dealt from: when Fruit & veg is listed, its items are in the stock for the machine's NEW ITEMS prizes, but the deal draws only from the staples and Cold cases, 11 products (the seed 99 profile). The screen marks such an aisle PRIZES ONLY so it doesn't look like a dealt pool.
  • Copies: at most 2 of a card, staples counted. The 2 Bananas are already the limit, so a Banana is never dealt. Only Banana bunch goes past it on purpose (Banana ×4, marked on the ticket).
  • Pins: pin_slots is 1 today (the toggle shows how 2 would look). The run copies the worn pins in StartChoice.new_run, before the choice. Today you change pins only in REWARDS › BADGE PINS. No pin has starting cards now (Recycle's two 2 for 1 coupons went with v0.90), so the cart's FROM YOUR PINS bracket shows only when a pin brings cards.

Questions for you (rules and flow; nothing here is decided)

  1. Q1 · Change pins on this screen? Today pins change only in REWARDS, and the run copies them before the choice. My proposed default is yes: CHANGE PINS opens the badge screen over the board, saves to the profile and re-wears the waiting run (RunPins.wear, then the run save). It does not change the deal, because the fair-deal check doesn't read pins. That is a flow change, so it needs your yes. If you say no, the panel is read-only and points to REWARDS.
  2. Q2 · The fair-deal check ignores pins. Express lane's −2 slots and Double checkout's two rows of 4 aren't in the check that guarantees a playable shift 1. Should the check see the worn pins? That would be a rule change, and with Q1 a pin change could then re-deal.
  3. Q3 · Listed machine aisles add nothing to the deal. With random_capsules off (TODO(S2)), a listed Fruit & veg shrinks the deal's pool to the staples plus one base aisle (11 products). The screen shows this honestly as PRIZES ONLY. Keep it, or turn random_capsules on?
  4. Q4 · After OPEN STORE: keep the short fly-in of the pack's cards and the deal's drop (today's 3 s, now that you've already seen everything), shorten it to the fold alone, or keep it only on run 1?
  5. Q5 · A pack hint is out of date. Bargain bin's hint says "Dented can pays +2 for each Clearance before it", but the card's rule is "+2 for each card redrawn this shift" (data/cards/dented_can.tres). The new screen puts the hint and the card's popup side by side, so the mismatch will show. Should the hint follow the card, or the card follow the hint? This may be one of the "3 stale pack hints" already noted under #128.
  6. Defaults I took (say if any is wrong): no re-deal and no seed field; the second pin slot is shown only when pin_slots is 2; the stock panel lists every aisle, locked ones too, as a progress hint; a pin's starting cards get their own bracket; the confirm reads OPEN STORE, with START SHIFT 1 in the sub line.
