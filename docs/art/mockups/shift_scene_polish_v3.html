4 · Tickets: a clean summary (family, price, effect)

Direction B (colour card, fixed price window, rule on a cream plate) at 108×76 (54×38 art px at 2x), as you asked. Why v2's icons felt odd:

  • The 9×9 family glyphs are small groceries: a milk carton, a loaf. They sat right under the item art on the belt, so they read as more groceries rather than as a label.
  • They were heavy ink badges with no margin. They touched the band's edges and pushed into the price window.
  • The inline glyph in the rule ("+1 per earlier [egg]") was taller than the words' capitals and sat off their baseline.
  • The struck mini price had no room, so "4€" read as "4E".

Three concepts take the pictures off the ticket in different ways. Two judges scored them, and the recommended ticket is the winner with the grafts both judges asked for.
