Rota card: weeks and days

A run is 12 shifts in 3 weeks of 4 days. At the start of each week a rota card shows the week event and all 4 days. Then it shrinks into the top bar as a week chip and a day chip. Green days help you, red days hurt, and day 4 of each week is the Manager's review with 2 maluses.

Mock-up for the 2026-10-28 decision. Event names and numbers are examples, not tuned.

1Week start, live

The card arrives, the week event stamps, the 4 days fill in, then the card flies into the top bar. Tap the week chip to open the card again. Next shift moves the day chip.

2Three card layouts

Same content, three looks. Each one plays its own arrival. They follow the week picked above.

A. Rota on a corkboard

+ Reads like a real staff rota; 4 days side by side show the week shape at a glance; fits the landscape Windows screen.
− On a phone the 4 columns drop to 2×2; the cork needs a new background asset.

B. Receipt strip

+ Reuses the receipt paper and the print-out motion players already know; a vertical list reads well on a phone.
− A third receipt next to the shift receipt and the machine tickets; it can blur with the score receipt.

C. Punch-clock time card RECOMMENDED

+ A cashier's own card: each shift start punches the day, so the card is also your progress. Vertical, so it works on phone and Windows. Different from the receipts.
− Needs one new frame (the card) and a punch sound; rows are less "week at a glance" than A.

Recommendation: C. It gives every shift start a small moment (the punch) and the week chip in the top bar can show the same punched pips.
