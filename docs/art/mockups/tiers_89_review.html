#89 item tiers: BIG and GOLDEN

Art only (#89, decided 2026-10-21): the 16 stocked products that had no tiers each get a BIG sprite, drawn from the plain one, and a GOLDEN sprite, a palette map of BIG through tools/pixel_art.py's @derive. Numbers, tier cards and making them winnable come later. Each tier is shown at 3x and 1x on cream and on teal. The page loads the real sprites from art/items_v2/, so it always shows the current art.

GOLDEN maps: rank (the item's shades over brass to gold, with a paper glint), yellow (a yellow item one step darker, as Banana), egg, and the new bake (only the brown ramp goes gold, so a bag, a tie and a loaf's cut crumb keep their colours), and keys, an item's own map, for the items the fixed maps made muddy or unreadable (each pair recolours one palette key; the glint keeps clear of signs and kept details).
