Redraw a basket v1.1 · live mock-up · 2026-10-22 · no game code, no rule changed

Your idea: a redraw takes a whole basket instead of 1 or 2 items you pick. Below is the real B2 mirrored shift screen (the game's own capture), with the hand, the baskets, the Redraw key and the monitor live on top, at the game's size. Play it with the mouse, or press Replay to watch the demo. Switch between Today and Basket redraw to compare. Start of planning has an empty belt and 8 items; Mid-planning is the game's capture as it is, 4 items on the belt and 4 left in the baskets.

What changes for the player

  • Fewer clicks: Redraw, then a basket: 2 clicks. Today it is Redraw, pick, pick, Confirm: 4 clicks.
  • It fits the baskets: the basket you click tips its items out toward the Cart and fresh ones pop up in it.
  • You lose a choice: one redraw can no longer swap two items from different baskets (that now costs both redraws).
  • You lose a choice: you can't swap one item out of a full basket, unless you first move its neighbour to the belt. With that belt trick, what you really lose is two items from different baskets in one redraw (and a few clicks).
  • The pairs matter now: which two items share a basket depends on the draw order, so a redraw can throw away a good item.
  • It fades as you plan: a basket redraw is strongest before you place anything (8 items, 4 full baskets). Each item you put on the belt leaves a gap, so by mid-planning most baskets hold 1 item or none (see Mid-planning).
This changes a rule. It makes redraws weaker, so the simulator has to measure it before anything is built. Today's win rate is 60.5% with 2 redraws, and that was measured before #77. The measure: basket redraw against item redraw, both at 2 redraws, with the #77 machine. The simulator first needs a redraw policy that chooses baskets (its greedy and sensible rows pick items today).

Defaults I chose (say no to any of them)

  • One redraw takes one basket, so at most 2 items, the same as today's limit. You still get 2 redraws a shift, and Extra redraw still adds 1.
  • Redraw mode ends after one basket. Press Redraw again for the next one.
  • An empty basket can't be clicked: it shows no tag and doesn't move.
  • If there are fewer items left to draw than the basket holds, the redraw swaps as many as it can, left item first, and the monitor tells you which item stays.
  • The swapped items fly into the Cart but stay set aside for the rest of the shift, as they do today (they come back next shift).
  • An item you take back from the belt returns to its own place: the basket it was drawn into.
  • When a basket can be redrawn, it shows a teal REDRAW tag on its front, the same teal as today's REDRAW flag. On hover the basket eases up 4 px and gives two small wobbles, then settles; the tag shows how many items go, and the monitor explains. The tag prints in Pixelify 12 px, a size the game prints cleanly.
  • The key reads Redraw 2 left, as today. In redraw mode it reads Pick a basket, and Cancel appears. With nothing left to draw it reads Redraw · none to draw.
  • The animation lasts about 1 s: the basket squashes, lifts and tips toward the Cart, the items slide out at full tip and arc into the Cart, the basket rights itself, and the new items pop up out of it and bounce. Reduced motion: a 0.3 s fade in place.
  • Coupons go back like any item.

3 questions

1. How do you start a basket redraw?

★ Redraw key, then click a basket. 2 clicks, it keeps the register key, and you can't spend a redraw by mistake when you meant to pick up an item.

Or: a tag on each basket (Trigger: Basket tag). 1 click and no key, but a slip of the mouse costs a redraw, and the key turns into a counter.

2. A basket with one item left?

★ Redraw just that item, for 1 redraw. This lets you keep the good item: move it to the belt, then redraw the basket.

Or: fill the basket back up to 2 (the hand grows by one, which changes a second rule), or don't allow it.

3. Should items stay in their basket?

★ Yes, they stay put. Today, when an item goes to the belt, the hand packs to the left, so every basket's pair changes. If the pairs are what you redraw, they shouldn't move. The game's core already keeps these places (the deck's hand never reorders; a redraw replaces in place), so this is a change to the view, not a new rule: an item taken back from the belt returns to its own place.

Or: keep packing (Hand: Packs left) and accept that the pairs shift as you plan. Try it in the "Mid-planning" scenario.
