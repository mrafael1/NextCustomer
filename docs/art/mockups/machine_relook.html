Machine relook: the rewards machine in the shift scene's art

The shift scene now uses a warm pixel-art store, with steel and brass trim and rivets, cream paper with ink, teal bands, hard pixel words, and prices written "80€". The rewards machine still has the v0.50 look. This page shows three relooks, drawn as full 1920×1080 frames at art px = 3 screen px, the way the game draws at 1080p. The backdrop is the game's own capture of the back wall. The sprites (sealed tickets, items, coupon slip, icons) are the game's PNGs from NC-machine/art.

The rules, odds, flow and timings stay the same. Every frame keeps the same visit: read, then choose a chute, then PUSH TO CONFIRM, then the R1 drop, then KEEP ONE with the SKIP bin, then the pick, then the cart (a product or coupon) or the loyalty card (a perk). The live loop plays it. The toolbar switches between the Classic and Premium visits, freezes one state, and turns on reduced motion (cuts instead of motion).

Captured with debug/screen_capture in NC-machine: godot --path . --resolution 1920x1080 res://debug/screen_capture/screen_capture.tscn -- --capture=<dir> --machine=3 (plus ,new_items · ,upgrade,premium · ,new_items,pick=0 · ,odds,new_items). The files are in docs/art/mockups/machine_relook_build/.

What clashes with the new shift scene
  • Pixel text at a non-integer scale. "READING…", "PICK 1", "Day-old buns", "Big Carrier bag", "Shelf upgrade" and the cart's title use Pixelify Sans as a smooth font at 24 stage px, which is 36 screen px. Its font pixels land on 1.5 to 2 screen px, so the strokes look uneven and soft. The shift scene's words are hard pixels (ArtStyle.use_pixel_text_material, 1 font px = 1 art px).
  • Small text. Several labels are 8 to 11 stage px (12 to 16.5 screen px) in caps: "ONE PRIZE PER RECEIPT", "ADD TO CART", "IMPROVE WHAT YOU OWN", "TAKE YOUR PRIZE", "COMMON / RARE / LEGENDARY" under the odds, "BONUS TICKET HERE" (8 px) and "or skip the draw". The shift scene's smallest body text is about 18 screen px.
  • Text on top of other text. While the prizes are armed, KEEP ONE is drawn over the tray's PUSH and TAKE YOUR PRIZE, and the SKIP bin sits on the plinth over "TAKE YOUR PRIZE". On the premium draw, the tilted ON YOUR LAST ROW strips cross the tickets' bottom edges.
  • Palette and trim. The cabinet is flat teal with a 2 px edge, and the marquee is a flat mustard block. Neither has the steel, brass, rivets or ink outline that the register tower, gauge housing and € plate use. The tray is flat grey. The premium gold trim is a 3 px line, not a brass part.
  • Button style. The NEW ITEMS and UPGRADE keys are flat cream panels with a thin base, and the PUSH flap is a cream strip. The shift scene's keys (CHECKOUT, Redraw) are keycaps with an ink outline, a light top edge, a bottom face and a press depth.
  • Dead space. At 1080p the cabinet takes the middle 672 px. About 1250 px of bare wall is left at the sides. The tray, vents and plinth take the bottom 27% and only say PUSH. Over the prizes the veil hides the whole cabinet, so the odds the player chose from are gone while they pick.
  • Tickets overhang the cabinet. Three 200-stage-px tickets (600 px) stand over a 448 px cabinet. They cover its edges and the vitrine's frame, so the machine reads as a backdrop for a popup instead of a machine handing out prizes.
  • Price format. "NEW ITEM · 2€" puts the price inline in the band's caps. The shift scene shows a price in a fixed price window (paper box, ink outline, number font). The euro already comes after the number everywhere.

2 · Three relooks, one visit

All three variants play the same visit on the same clock. Use Premium in the toolbar for the boosted face, LOYALTY and UPGRADE prizes, and the end state where the stamp flies to the top bar. The state buttons freeze a beat. 1:1 shows real 1080p pixels.

V1

Faithful relook: same layout, new skin and legibility

  • Geometry unchanged: the cabinet stays at 448×568 at MACHINE_RECT, and the tickets stay 200×264 and float over it. Only the skin changes: a 2-stage-px ink outline, a steel-lipped vitrine with rivets, a brass marquee plate, an LED display with hard pixel words, keycaps with a press depth, and a steel tray with a brass push bar.
  • Fixes: KEEP ONE moves onto the push bar (no overlap), the odds plates show the % only with one rarity legend on the sill, and every word is hard pixel at 2x or 4x, or Atkinson at 12 stage px or more.
  • Leaves: the dead space, and tickets that overhang the cabinet.
V2

Steel-and-brass cabinet in the left column's language

RECOMMENDED
  • A bolder cabinet: 560×584 stage px, 112 px wider and 16 px taller than today. Steel side pillars use the gauge housing's caps and rivets, the marquee is an engraved brass plate like the € plate, a teal band runs under it, and the body is dark teal. The premium transform turns the pillars to brass (the trim runs down them) and the sign to a night face with chasing bulbs.
  • The prizes land inside the cabinet: three 168×248 tickets cover the vitrine and the key plate and stay within the pillars. KEEP ONE lights the push bar, and the SKIP bin stands between the sunk keys. The veil darkens only the parts behind the tickets.
  • Legibility: names are hard pixel at 2x (wrapped over 2 lines when needed), rules are Atkinson 600 at 12 stage px, prices are in a price window, and the odds are hard pixel numbers on LED plates.
V3

Prize counter: a wide machine with a prize bay beside the odds

  • My idea for the dead space: a 1040×584 counter machine. The left module is the vitrine, keys and tray. The right module is a lit prize bay with three pedestals, where the tickets land. The odds stay readable while you pick (no veil), and the SKIP bin lives in the bay.
  • Cost: it changes MACHINE_WALL_RECT (520×292 on the back wall instead of 224×284), the camera crop, the flight paths, and the cart drawer's anchor. It is the biggest build of the three.

3 · Side by side, frozen on the prizes

Recommendation: V2, the steel-and-brass cabinet
  • It uses the left column's language. Steel housings with caps and rivets, an engraved brass plate, an ink outline and teal bands make the machine look like it comes from the same store as the register tower and the gauge.
  • It fixes the layout bugs without changing the flow. The tickets fit inside the pillars, KEEP ONE sits on the push bar instead of over it, and the bin has a place of its own. Every beat, wait point and MachineTuning time stays as it is.
  • Every word is crisp at 1080p. Pixel words are hard at 2x or 4x, body text is 12 stage px or more, and no caps label is under 14 stage px.
  • The build stays small. It keeps the same parts (MachineCabinet, MachineHead, MachineDisplay, StockWindow, ChuteKey, MachineTray, PrizeTicket, SkipBin) with new rects and drawing. The camera glide still lands at a net scale of 1. V3 would also move the wall rect, the crop and the flights.
  • Why not V1: it keeps the overhang and the dead bottom quarter. Why not V3: it is the better use of 1080p width, but it is a new layout, and a back-wall machine 520 px wide covers two shelf bays. It is worth doing only if you want the odds visible while picking.
