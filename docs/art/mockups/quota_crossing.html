What each idea does

  • AFlag on the item. The item whose count passes the quota gets the gauge's own flag planted on it: a mustard pennant reading SPEND MET ✓. The player sees it where they're looking (the scanner), and it says this item did it. It stays on the item until it's bagged.
  • BHit-stop + streak. The game freezes for 0.13 s and the camera dips 3 px, with a warm flash. A gold spark flies from the item to the quota mark on the gauge, and the gauge pulses when it lands. Your eye travels to the top-left corner at the one moment it matters.
  • CKa-ching. A one-off register bell at the crossing, apart from the counting ladder. The real game would use a CC0 recording through make_sfx.py. This one is synthesised in the page.
  • DBonus lights. After the crossing, the belt's front rail lights up in chasing gold for the rest of the count. Everything past this point is extra, and the scene says so. In game this could also lift the count's pitch ladder one step.
  • E×2 pop (revised). Nothing extra at the quota itself: the gauge behaves as it does now until the total reaches twice the quota (quota €40 → pops at €80). Then the bar flashes white, banks in gold, a big ×2 stamp slams onto the gauge with sparkles and a bell, and settles as the flag at the bar's end; the tag turns to "€N to ×3" and the next lap fills from there (×3, ×4 …, each popping the same way). This clip ends at €147, short of ×2 (€160), so for E only the page pretends ×2 is €146 and pops it on the last item.
