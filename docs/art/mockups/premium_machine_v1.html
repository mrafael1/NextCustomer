Premium machine v1

The RECEIPT REWARDS machine, redrawn front-on: the classic receipt (NEW ITEMS / COUPONS), the shift that announces a premium receipt, three ways the same machine turns PREMIUM (LOYALTY / UPGRADES), and the prize tickets with the real sprites and the rarity effects. Every premium face keeps for the whole visit: chasing bulbs round the sign, a thick gold cabinet trim, the gold receipt sticking out of its slot, gold pedestals in the stock window, a gold tray and gold keys. A / B / C differ in the move and in the colour of the sign and window. Shown at an exact 2x (1x on narrow screens). Slow motion runs at a quarter speed.

Premium dressing (scene 3, and the look of scene 4 for Loyalty / Upgrades)

Shared by all three end states: chasing bulbs round the sign, 3 px gold trim (highlight, gold, shadow) down both cabinet edges, the gold receipt left in the slot, gold pedestals under the prizes, a gold tray and flap, gold keys. The costs below come on top of the classic redraw.

A: night sign + gold trim

The cheap one. The bulbs chase on, the mustard sign flashes to a dark "night" face with lit gold letters, and the gold trim runs down both edges with a spark at its tip. The four compartments restock one by one onto gold pedestals, the NEW ITEMS / COUPONS keys sink and gold keys rise on a dark plate. The window and body stay teal: the change is the sign, the trim and the gold parts.

Build cost: about 1 day on top of the classic. Bulbs are one 3x3 sprite placed in code with a lit flag; the trim is two 9-slice strips grown by a tween; second textures for the sign, plate, pedestals, flap and keys; the restock reuses the drop tween.

B: velvet panel flip MY PICK

Like a shop sign turned round: the sign flips (full, half, edge, back, in hard steps) to a velvet-and-gold "★ PREMIUM ★" face, each compartment spins round onto velvet with its premium prize, then the key plate flips to velvet with gold keys. Each flip lands with a clack. Crimson plus gold is the clearest "better" at a squint, and it is a different colour family from the mustard-and-teal classic.

Build cost: 1 to 1.5 days on top of the classic. Flips are a stepped scale tween with the texture swapped at the edge frame (no 3D); needs the velvet sign, window back, plate and gold key textures, plus the clack sound.

C: gold flood into the tank

The monitor's win language: liquid gold rises in the stock window, every prize it passes turns premium, and the window back turns dark brass behind it. When it reaches the top the sign pops to the banded gold face (monitor FONT C) with a short sunburst and the coin fountain, which clear within a second. The gold then drains down into a tank in the kick panel, which stays full and bubbling for the whole visit.

Build cost: about 1.5 days on top of the classic. The liquid is the monitor tank's polygon (ui/gold_tank.gd) pointed at the window and the kick-panel tank; the sunburst and coins are ui/tank_burst.gd; the per-prize swap is a texture swap when the level passes its centre.

Rarity reveal (scene 4)

R1: drop, effect on landing MY PICK

The tray light flickers through grey / teal / gold while packing (a hint of the best prize). Tickets fly out of the tray to the front, the legendary one last and a beat later. COMMON: clean drop, 2 px squash, dust. RARE: teal glow ring, one hard 3 px shine band, four sparkles, a two-note chime. LEGENDARY: white flash, a dithered sunburst that turns and shrinks to a small glow within a second, coins, orbiting sparkles, a 0.3 s shake, the LEGENDARY!! tab punches in, a short sting.

Build cost: about 1 day. Rays and coins come from ui/tank_burst.gd; the glow ring is a 9-slice; the shine is a masked 3 px strip; sparkles are art/effects/sparkle.png.

R2: face-down, flip one by one

All three land face down (teal backs, gold backs on a premium visit) and flip left to right in hard steps. The legendary one trembles for 0.6 s first, with gold light leaking round its edges, then flips straight into the R1 legendary burst. More suspense, about 1.2 s longer.

Build cost: R1 plus about 0.5 day: ticket backs (2 textures), the flip tween and the tremble.

The other scenes

1 Announce (shift 3 planning)

At the start of the shift before a premium receipt, a gold receipt prints down the hung monitor: "NEXT RECEIPT ★ PREMIUM ★", the two premium keys' real icons, "MEET THIS SHIFT'S QUOTA". It then shrinks and flies up to hang on a string to the right of the quota flag, clear of the bar's marker and the monitor pole, and glints every couple of seconds for the whole shift.

Build cost: about 0.5 day: one receipt panel reused from the results print-out, one tag texture, a tween.

2 Classic (Normal visit), the base redraw

The camera glides to the machine, a white receipt feeds into the slot beside the LED, READING..., and the two big keys light up: NEW ITEMS and COUPONS. Each key's two stocked prizes stand on pedestals in the columns right above it. No locked keys: the premium keys only exist on a premium visit. The tray is a short slot, so the stock window gets the height.

Build cost: 3 to 4 days. This is a rework of reward_machine_view, not a reskin: the box lane, box lids, cart drawer and restock hood (ui/machine/box_lane.gd, cart_drawer.gd, art/machine/box*.png, restock_hood.png) give way to the stock window and pedestals; the machine body becomes PNG parts at the one front-on view; the small version on the back wall (seen during planning) is redrawn to match; and the camera framing is fixed so the tray is not cropped.

What is real here

Real: the item, coupon, icon, coin, stamp and sparkle PNGs from art/, the palette from art/src/palette.txt, the fonts and font roles, the prize names and rule texts from data/ (shortened). New: the machine's shapes (drawn here in code on the 2 px grid, as a guide for the PNG parts), the loyalty punch card (the HUD's stamp slots on a card, one stamped with the perk's icon), the ticket backs, the gold receipt and tag. The coupon slip shows its icon (2 for 1, Multipack) or its name (Clearance tag), as in the game. Shelf upgrade shows plain Milk on a gold shelf, since the GOLDEN palette on Milk reads as a different product.
