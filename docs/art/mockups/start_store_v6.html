YOUR STORE, redrawn: three baskets, four regulars, nothing else until you earn it

Your verdict on the shipped page: too much going on, nothing intuitive. The page does three jobs: it shows today's baskets (6 items dealt from the Staples shelf and today's two STARTER aisles), it shows your regulars (the 4 items in every run), and it holds three unlocks (regular swaps from 1 completed aisle, RESTOCK from 3, choosing your aisles from 6). The redraw keeps those jobs, drops the always-on aisle board, legend, source rail, tip card and per-basket RESTOCK tags, and makes every control appear in place, only once it is unlocked.

1 · Live: pick a direction and how far the player has got

Everything on the stage can be clicked. At 3 or 6: press the yellow RESTOCK, then click a basket (or 1, 2, 3; Esc cancels). At 2 or more: click a regular to swap it (↺ puts it back). At 6: click a basket's aisle sign (the ⇄ tab) to change that aisle. Hover any item for its rule. The red NEW tag marks what the chosen step has just unlocked.

2 · The three directions

A

Counter

RECOMMENDED

Still two pages. On page 1, three hand baskets hang under their aisle signs on one rail, with today's extra (RUN ONLY) aisle as a small sign at the end of the rail, and the regulars stand on a shelf below. A new player sees 6 + 4 items and NEXT, nothing to read. Each unlock adds one thing in place: a ⇄ badge on each regular, one RESTOCK button by the baskets, a ⇄ tab on the two starter signs.

B

Three pages

STORE › REGULARS › BUILD. Page 1 is only the baskets, bigger, with RESTOCK under them. Page 2 shows the regulars large with their rules printed under each, and the swap shelf opens below. The REGULARS tab is hidden until the first completed aisle, so a new player still has two pages. The roomiest, but every run from then on costs one more page.

C

Cart strip

The page is your starting cart: one belt with 15 slots: 4 regulars, 3 baskets of 2, then 5 dashed slots for the pack you pick next. Labels over each group hold the actions. A big info panel under the belt reads out the item you hover. It says "this is what you start with" most plainly, but the items are small and it repeats page 2's ORDER SHEET.

Recommendation: A, Counter. It keeps the two-page flow, so nothing gets slower. The page shows real store objects you already know from the shift (the hand basket, the aisle signs, the shelf), not a table. Each unlock is one extra control on the thing it changes, so there is nothing to learn before you have it. B is a good fallback if A still feels crowded once the regulars' rules have to be readable. C is better as an idea for page 2's order sheet than as page 1.
