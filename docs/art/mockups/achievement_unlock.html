The unlock card: the moment you earn something that stays

Some things stay with your profile after a run: badge pins (earned by milestones like "Finish a run") and, with the new start screen, completed aisles (every item of an aisle collected: its base items plus its figurines), which unlock regular swaps, RESTOCK and choosing your aisles. Today the game unlocks them silently: the pin just shows as owned the next time you open the badge screen. This page designs the card that announces each unlock, and how it appears.

It appears on the results screen (the "store closed" receipt) for pins, and on the TOY MACHINES screen when an aisle's last figurine comes out. The backdrops below are sketches of those screens; only the card and its motion are being designed. Click the stage (or Skip) to do what a click does in the game; the receipt's buttons can be clicked too.

1 · Live: pick a look, a reveal and a scene

2 · The three card looks

The same unlock (Recycle, the pin for finishing a run) and the same aisle completion in each look. All three use the game's pin face as drawn today (PinFace: ink rim, dark band, face colour, glint, the pin's letters in the number font).

A

Pin card

RECOMMENDED

The pin comes on a shop backing card, like the pins sold on a hook by the till: a hang slot, a coloured header, the pin in a clear blister, the rule, then a green line with the milestone you met. It is something a supermarket really sells, so it fits the store and reads as a collectible at once.

B

Staff notice

A note from the manager pinned up with a tack, the pin on it and an APPROVED stamp. Warm and funny, close to the receipt's paper, but it reads as a message more than as a thing you own.

C

Plaque

An "employee of the month" plaque: wood frame, velvet, an engraved brass plate. The most prestigious, but heavy for a small unlock, and engraved plates leave little room for the rule. It has no stamp: where the reveals say "the stamp lands", the name plate flashes paper-white instead.

3 · The three reveals

R1

Receipt stub

RECOMMENDED

The final receipt prints one more line after the coins, ★ NEW PIN Recycle. The card slides out from behind the receipt at that line and settles in the empty space on the right. The pin pops into its blister, the stamp lands, a few sparkles. Each card is a line on the receipt, so it belongs to the run you just played. On a receipt too long for the screen, the ★ lines fold into one (★ 3 NEW PINS) and the cards come from it one by one.

R2

Drop in

The card falls from the top of the screen into the right-hand space, bounces once and puffs a little dust. Simple and punchy, but it does not say where the unlock came from.

R3

Spotlight

The screen dims and the card arrives face down in the centre, flips over, waits for a click (or 1.6 s) and slides to the side. The biggest moment, but it covers the receipt and stops you several times after a run that unlocks 3 things.

Recommended: A Pin card with R1 Receipt stub

  • A is the only look that is clearly an object you now own, and the badge pin is exactly that. The same backing card works for an aisle (its sign colour and glyph) and for a start-screen tool (dark mustard).
  • R1 reuses the receipt's own printing rhythm (one line every 80 ms, a click prints the rest), so the unlock reads as part of the run's reward, like the coins. It never covers the receipt and takes about 1.4 s per card, with no extra click.
  • Several unlocks stack in the right-hand space (the newest on top, the older ones peeking above it), with "1 / 3" and arrows to look back. The buttons keep today's rule: armed 350 ms after the screen appears; clicking one while cards are still coming first shows them all in place, then does its job.

4 · Every card that can appear, in look A

Only unlocks that exist in the game today, or are already decided for the new start screen. Decks and card variants have the unlock code but no content yet (after launch), and the profile's achievements list is empty until Steam achievements (phase 4), so none of them get a card here.

Not in this pass: a card for UNLOCK A RANDOM PIN (12 coins) on the badge screen, which is now a popup on the start screen's YOUR BUILD page; that pin keeps today's silent unlock, since it shows as owned right there. A title or start-screen place for cards left unseen is not designed either (see "Never lost").

Timing per cardThe first ★ line prints 300 ms after the coin total (the receipt's 80 ms line rhythm before it). The card starts 60 ms after its line and lasts 1000 ms: 0–240 ms it slides out from behind the receipt's right edge at that line's height to its place (ease out, 2 px steps); 240–440 ms the pin grows into the blister in whole art px to 125 % and back; 460–580 ms the stamp lands from 220 % to 100 %, its lettering stepping 1 art px at a time, then shakes 2 art px for 120 ms; 500–1000 ms four sparkles blink. The next ★ line prints 300 ms after the card ends: about 1.36 s per card. Idle: a glint crosses the pin every 3 s. Sound: the receipt's print tick, a soft pop, the stamp family's thump.
ClicksToday's rule stays (results_panel.gd, ARM_MS 350): New run and Toy machines › arm 350 ms after the screen appears. A tap on the receipt prints the rest and shows every remaining card at once in its final state. A click on New run or Toy machines › while cards are coming does the same first, then does its job, as Toy machines already prints the rest of the receipt today. When everything is shown, ‹ › or a click on the stack brings the next card to the top.
Reduced motionEach card appears at its line in its final place, with no slide, pop, shake, sparkle, dim or flip; the glint is off. So R1, R2 and R3 look the same with it on.
Never lostThe unlock is saved at the checkout click (as today). A card counts as seen once it has landed. If the game closes, or a button leaves the screen, before a card landed, the profile keeps its id as unseen and the next results or TOY MACHINES screen that shows cards shows it first. NEW (one saved list of unseen ids). A title or start-screen place for them is not designed here.

6 · What the game does today

  • Badge pins (data/pins/, 8 in catalogue.tres): Recycle (Finish a run, kind FINISH_RUNS), Repack (Win a run, WIN_RUNS) and Double checkout (Score €100 in one checkout) have milestones; Stock list, Day off, Golden basket, Price gun and Express lane have none yet, so they are owned from the start (FULL_BUILD_PLAN.md 7.4).
  • Milestones are checked when a run ends, won or lost, in ProfileState.record_run, called at the last checkout click in ui/shift_screen.gd. Its returned list is not used: plan 7.4 says "nor does the results screen show an unlock notice".
  • The coin fallback, UNLOCK A RANDOM PIN for 12 coins (pin_coin_price), is on the badge screen, which now also opens from the start screen's YOUR BUILD page (P key). The BADGE PINS card in REWARDS is approved for removal (2026-11-05), not built yet.
  • Results buttons: New run and Toy machines › can be clicked mid-print once armed (350 ms); Toy machines first prints the rest of the receipt. The receipt prints a dim line under a shift for each machine visit ("NEW ITEMS: Milk", "BONUS · UPGRADE: Eggs › GOLDEN") and the inspection played, and steps its sizes down to fit.
  • Aisles: the TOY MACHINES draw figurines (1 coin each), one machine per aisle. A full aisle shows ALL COLLECTED on the FIGURINE SHELF; the machines' LED strip shows only its hint, 0 coins, OUT OF ORDER or, at 30 / 30, COLLECTION COMPLETE. On the new start screen (uncommitted, core/start_setup_rules.gd) an aisle is complete when every item in it is collected (base items count, so Cold cases, Pantry and Household need only 2 or 3 figurines); each completed aisle gives one regular swap (at most 4), 3 completed give RESTOCK, 6 give the choice of aisles. Nothing announces these yet.
  • Decks and card variants have UnlockCondition support but no locked content; ProfileState.achievements exists and is empty ("their content comes later"); Steam achievements are phase 4.

Questions (each has a default)

  1. Look and reveal? Default: A Pin card + R1 Receipt stub.
  2. Results buttons: keep today's rule (armed at 350 ms; a click first shows the rest of the cards, then acts) or make New run wait until the last card has landed? Waiting would be NEW: about 1.4 s more per card, 4 s on a run with 3 pins. Default: keep today's rule.
  3. Aisle completion cards on the TOY MACHINES screen, right after the last figurine lands? Default: yes. (The figurine shelf animation itself is its own mock-up.)
  4. Show the next milestone under the stack ("Next: Repack · Win a run")? Default: yes, for pins only, hidden once every pin is owned. (No separate page of every card earned for now: the badge screen and the figurine shelf already list what you own.)
