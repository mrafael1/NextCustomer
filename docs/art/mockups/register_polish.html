Register polish

Three things on the cash register at the bottom left: what its screen shows while the belt counts, the odd ink lines on its frame, and the coins flying into the drawer. Every strip below runs on a loop with the same checkout (6 items, quota €40) and the game's own drawer and coin art.

Your system asks for reduced motion: the strips still play here so you can judge them; in the game they would hold still.

1 · The screen

My verdict: today it only repeats. While counting it shows TOTAL €5 and "SPEND €80 · €75 TO GO". The SPEND gauge in the top bar already shows both, bigger, and the monitor explains each item. So the screen is the third place telling you the same number.

It should count something nothing else counts. Three new jobs below. All of them keep today's SPEND MET flash at the quota.

Same number as the gauge, same "to go" as the gauge's tag.

Tally

MY PICK

Bonuses fired, wasted, items left. It counts the combos as they fire and keeps the WASTED fizzles that otherwise vanish from the belt. Tells you how good the row really was, not only how much it paid.

Pole display

A real till's line display: each item scrolls in with its price, the one before it above. Very "supermarket", but it overlaps the receipt.

Overtime

Dark until the quota, then OVERTIME. It counts what you make past the quota and how far the gauge's ×2 lap is. Before the quota it has nothing to say.

2 · The frame lines

What's wrong: the register's front starts at the screen edge, but its 9-slice frame draws an ink line a few pixels in, so a black stripe runs down the left with a sliver of dark wall beside it. A second ink line runs across above the column, cut short on the right. Together they read as a box glued to the edge.

The fix I'd make: the cabinet runs off the left side of the screen with no left outline, like the belt runs off the right. One clean top edge (a steel lip, as on the printer box) and a 2 px ink outline only on the sides you can see. It's a straight fix, so no choice is needed unless you'd rather keep a full frame.

3 · Coins into the drawer

Today: five 10 px coins slide on the same small curve all at once, and the drawer nudges down 4 px (18 px at the quota). It reads as a twitch, not a till taking money. Both versions below have the drawer open (sliding out to show its coin tray), use the game's coin at full 12 px size, scale with the payout, and snap the drawer shut on the last coin.

A

Chunky arc + snap

1 to 3 big coins (2x) per item by payout, each on a high arc with a short trail. The drawer kicks open with an overshoot, each coin lands with a squash and a spark, the drawer slams shut and the € on its face flashes gold. Punchy, one hit per item.

B

Coins stream in

One small coin per €2 (up to 10), poured one after another into a drawer that stays open. Each lands with a tick and a 1 px jiggle, a +€N tag counts up on the drawer, and it closes after the last one. Big payouts feel big, small ones stay quick.
