Crisps

The crimped top was 6 rows tall with a pinched neck under it, so the bag read as a jar with a lid. Now the seal is 3 rows, the bag is as wide as the seal and taller than wide, and a short crimp closes the bottom too. On BIG, the packet behind now has a thin shine line, so it reads as a packet and not a red box.

Banana

Where the fingers met the stalk was a tangle of green and mustard pixels. Now the stalk flows into one brown crown and the fingers' green necks flow out of it, with no dark line cutting them apart, so it reads as one bunch and not a nut stuck on top.

Banana round 3

The round-2 top still read as a long thin stick poked into the bunch. Three other tops, same fruit bodies, palette, outline and size. V1 stem: the necks meet in one stubby crown under a short, thick green stem (4 px tall) with a dark cut tip. V2 curved: a compact crown and a shorter stalk, thick at the base, tapering as it curves up and leans back. V3 tips: no stalk; each finger's green neck ends in its own dark brown tip, the tips meeting in a small stepped crown. GOLDEN is built from each BIG through the same yellow map as today.
