Checkout rhythm

Version 2's register display, refined from your review. The belt carries each item to the scanner and a paid item hops into the bag beside it. A giver that has already been bagged shows on its monitor line, where its mini pulses. The pacing follows what matters: plain items pass quickly, and the multipliers and big pays take the time.

What changed from v2

  1. The belt carries the row. At each item the belt advances one slot, so the item reaches the scanner with its ticket, as it does in the game today. The scanner beam sweeps it and the monitor shows it. Once paid, it makes a short hop into the bag next to the scanner, and its ticket drops off.
  2. Bagged givers stay readable. A giver still on the belt hops. A giver already in the bag (the Banana before a chain, the Multipack for the later Bananas) can't, so its mini pulses on the monitor line, "from the bag" is written beside its name, and the bag gives a small nudge. Loyalty perks flip in the top bar and pulse on their line too.
  3. Variable pacing. The time goes to what changes the score. A plain item rides, beeps and pays in well under a second, and adds are quick taps. Multipliers get a heavier stamp and a held beat, and a pay waits in proportion to its size. The last pay and the spend get the longest holds.
  4. The total lives on the register. The CHECKOUT key turns into the customer display during the count, coins drop into the drawer on each pay, and SPEND MET flashes there. The monitor is never covered.
