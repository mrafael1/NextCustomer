The rhythm

  1. The item steps up. It lifts on the belt, the scanner beeps, and the monitor shows it with its effect.
  2. Each bonus fires from its giver. A loyalty perk flips in the top bar. The Multipack hops and its brass rail lights up to the item. For a Banana chain, the Banana before it hops. Then the price gun re-stamps the sticker it gave, with a little dust, and its amount floats off the sticker.
  3. The monitor adds a line per bonus and never drops one: giver, name, amount. The gold price ticks up on each line. Five lines fit in the wider column; a sixth would fold the earliest adds into one "+N earlier" line.
  4. The item pays. Its ticket's price punches, the item hops into the bag, and the money leaves for the total, wherever the total lives.
  5. The scanner beep climbs a note per bonus and the pay rings like a till. The spend ding happens where the total is, so the monitor never gets covered.

Where the total goes

  • 1 · Receipt + gauge. The monitor has no TOTAL. The receipt keeps a SUBTOTAL foot under the printing lines, and each pay flies a "+€N" chip into the top-bar gauge. At the spend, the gauge's flag flips, the bar turns green, and the receipt gets a SPEND MET stamp.
  • 2 · Register display. During the count the CHECKOUT key (unusable then anyway) becomes the register's customer display, with a big total and the spend line under it. Each pay drops coins into the cash drawer. At the spend, the display flashes SPEND MET and the drawer kicks open. The top-bar gauge follows quietly, as it does today.

Open questions
