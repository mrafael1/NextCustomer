The rhythm

  1. One beat at a time, in score order. The item steps up and shows its price, then each bonus fires in the scorer's order (adds, then multipliers), then the item pays. Nothing else moves during a beat.
  2. The giver moves. A belt item or ticket wiggles, or a loyalty perk wiggles in the top bar, and the bonus pops at the giver. The sticker it gave the item punches and gets a ✓.
  3. Two boxes, one sum. Adds go into the teal price box, multipliers into the gold × box, and the pay is price × mult. The scorer already applies every add before any multiplier, so the screen math is the real math.
  4. Nothing disappears early. Every bonus the item got stays on the monitor until it pays. The receipt keeps the full text.
  5. Rising tempo. Inside an item, each bonus comes a little faster and beeps a note higher. The pay is the loudest beat, and the spend ding gets a stamp.

A or B

  • A · Price × mult. The closest to Balatro. The monitor carries the sum (€21 × 6) and one chip per bonus. The caption names the bonus that just fired. This is the clearest to read, but it says less on the monitor.
  • B · Full ledger. Every line stays: base, each bonus with its giver, then the pay. Six lines fit. A longer list would fold its earliest adds into one "+N earlier" line. This is closer to today's monitor, without lines replacing each other.
  • Both keep the receipt, the scanner bay and the TOTAL column as they are now. The giver wiggles and pop-ups are the same in both.
