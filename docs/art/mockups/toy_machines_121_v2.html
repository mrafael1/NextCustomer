Toy machines v2: title art direction

Issue #121, revised after three reviews. The exit toy machines screen in the title's C+ pixel art: one 640x360 art scene at 2x, the store after closing, hard Pixelify words, number-font digits on the same pixel grid, shelf-label plates with brass ledges, palette colours only. Each aisle has its own cabinet on one shared base, with its figurine standing on a plinth above it. Point at a machine (or tap it, or use the arrow keys on the scene) to see that aisle's lineup.

State

Point at a machine or a button. On a touch screen, tap a machine to show its lineup. The Hover state pins Fruit & veg so it can be compared.

One machine at 3x

1 art px = 6 screen px. The cabinet top changes per aisle; the collar, body, coin slot, dial, price LED, chute and foot are the same on all six.

What changed after the reviews

  • Lit means you can play it. A machine you can buy from has its lamp on, a lit-on-hover sign and full colour. Out of coins, collected and out of order machines lose the lamp, take the off plates, and switch to a palette map two steps down each ramp (no haze blend, so every pixel is a palette colour).
  • Every word sits on something. The hint, the notices and COLLECTION COMPLETE sit on one LED strip (the title's coin-LED look) between the floor plates and the buttons. Digits are the number font sampled onto the same 2 px grid as the letters.
  • Quieter shared body. Smaller steel-shade dial, no glyph badge. The price is a small coin LED on the body, inside the 72 px footprint. Out of coins it shows a padlock and the slot gets its lid.
  • Capsules read as capsules. One shape everywhere: the aisle colour, an ink seam with a cream band, a paper "?". Each cabinet places them its own way: fridge shelves, a jar on a shelf, a tumbled drum, crates, oven trays, one per vending coil.
  • Stronger cabinets. The washer is cream enamel with suds in a teal drum. The jar has a gingham cap, a pantry label and a shelf. The fridge is white enamel with a cold light, wire shelves and frost. The vending box has one calm stripe, and the crisps stand on a dark plinth.
  • Hover like the title. The sign row stays still: the hovered sign lights, the cabinet and figurine rise 2 px, the lamp burns hotter and the floor plate takes the tick. There's no rim. A machine you can't play shows the lineup only. The lineup uses the real minis at 2x, and the floor plate carries the only count.
  • Removed: the Day scene, the clickable EXIT (now an unlit lightbox; DONE is the only way out), the unexplained NEW sticker and the ALL COLLECTED stamps. A collected machine has a green tick tag over its slot instead.

States at a glance

Out of coinsLamps off, slot lid shut, a padlock on the price LED, off plates, a tomato 0 on the COINS LED and a warning icon on the strip.

Whole collectionAll six collected, plus COLLECTION COMPLETE on the LED strip at 2x.

Open questions

  1. Should a collected machine keep its figurine in colour? I recommend yes: the topper becomes a trophy over a dark machine. The alternative dims it with the rest, which makes a finished aisle look like an empty one.
  2. Ham ends or Cream for Cold cases? One review read the ham as a ball at a glance. The fridge cabinet now says Cold cases by itself, so I recommend keeping Ham ends, as you chose. Cream is the only other Cold cases item and read as a spray bottle in v1.
