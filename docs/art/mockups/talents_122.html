Meta talents: what to decide before the prototype

Persistent talents equipped before a run, starting with one slot and at most three, and three first talents to test: two starting coupons, a full-basket redraw and Double checkout. Four questions below need your answer; everything after them is a default you can change.

Q1

When does it get built?

The plan has no talents: phase 3 ends at a content freeze (#69), and decks and variants wait for after launch. Talents would be a new system.

RecommendedPhase 3 prototype

Build one slot and the three talents now, behind the decisions below, and judge them in play before the content freeze. Slots 2 and 3 only if the prototype earns them.

After launch

Write the design into the plan and keep phase 3 on #123 and the freeze. Talents arrive with the post-launch decks.

The prototype is small (one slot, three talents) and the balance pass is still ahead, so it can be measured with the rest.

Q2

What are they called, and how do they look?

"Loyalty" already names the in-run perks (the loyalty card, the LOYALTY drops). Talents need their own name and their own object, so nobody confuses the two.

RecommendedBadge pins

The cashier's name badge holds 1 to 3 pins. Round metal pins, gold when equipped, so they never read as a stamp.

Staff perks

Plain and clear, but "perk" is already the loyalty card's word in the code and the plan.

Training certificates

Framed certificates on the staff-room wall. Fits "new ways to play", but it's a heavier object to show on screen.

Q3

How do you unlock them?

Coins already pay for the toy machines, and the plan's unlock rule is "options, not raw power".

RecommendedMilestones only

Each pin and each extra slot unlocks from a milestone, using the unlock conditions the game already checks at a run's end: win with a card, score €Y in one checkout, use a coupon N times. Phase 4 mirrors them as Steam achievements.

Coins

Pins cost coins like capsules. Gives coins a use after the collection, but it competes with the toy machines and turns pins into a shop.

Both

A milestone shows the pin; coins buy it. Two steps for one unlock.

Q4

What is Double checkout?

The riskiest talent: it touches the refill, the chains, the quota, inspections and the count-up. The shape decides most of those rules.

RecommendedTwo short rows
Row 1
refillhand back up to 8
Row 2
Row 1 + Row 2 → quota

Every shift has two checkouts of 4 slots instead of one of 7. The hand refills between them, and both totals count toward the quota. Combos must fit 4 slots, and the refill brings fresh options. About the same number of slots (8 against 7).

Second wind
Row 1
Row 2

Once per shift, after a checkout that misses the quota, refill and play a second full row, but the quota rises 50%. A rescue tool more than a new way to play.

Split hand
Row 1
Row 2

Two rows of 4 from one hand of 8, no refill. The simplest rules, but you plan both rows at once, so it plays like one row with a gap.

Defaults

Everything else, unless you say otherwise

  • Where you equip: the title's REWARDS menu, in place of the locked LOYALTY (SOON) card, and the REWARDS bay's poster becomes the badge. The cart reveal shows the equipped pin at a run's start. Changing pins mid-run is not allowed.
  • Slots and talents: one slot at first, three at most; there are always more pins than slots, so equipping is a choice.
  • "Two starting Bundles": read as two 2 for 1 coupons in the starting deck (Bundle became 2 for 1 after the prototype; the old Bundle card is out of every pool). Say so if you meant the old connector.
  • Full-basket redraw: once per shift, redraw all four baskets at once. It costs one redraw and works only while you have one left.
  • Double checkout (two short rows): row 1's cards are spent; the refill draws from the deck back up to 8, and redraws are shared across the shift. Chains and adjacency reset between rows. Inspections apply to each row (Spot check zeroes each row's 3rd slot; Short belt takes a slot from row 2). Slot-numbered rules count across both rows (Slot engine's slot 6 is row 2's 2nd; Opening deal is each row's first). The count-up plays row 1, then row 2, on one receipt, with one dessert at the end. The game saves after row 1, so quitting never replays it. Both rows count toward the quota and the bonus.
  • Simulator: --talents=<ids> runs with pins equipped, like --profile, for the end-of-phase balance pass.
  • The toy-machine loot idea (a capsule after a complete aisle turns an item GOLDEN for good): not now. It breaks the plan's rule "no permanent payout increases". A cosmetic alternative fits the rule: a complete aisle's machine sells gold figurines for the shelf.
