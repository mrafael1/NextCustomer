NOW · capped
What you see: the number pops, and a long number pops less, so it never leaves the paper or covers the bay.
BEFORE THE CAP · ×1.5 total, up to ×1.7 payout
What you'd see: every number pops just as much, so a long one covers the bay and spills past the screen's edge for a moment.

The numbers: the TOTAL pops to ×1.5. A payout pops to 1.2 + payout ÷ 25, up to ×1.7 for a payout of €12.5 or more (the 9€ case's €4 payout pops to ×1.36). The pop's ease overshoots by a tenth, so they peak at ×1.55 and at most ×1.77. The cap is the room right of the bay (the green dashed box, 274 px wide, centred on the number), so a capped pop peaks exactly at that room: about ×1.6 for 56, ×1.34 for 182, ×1.2 for 400 or 1118. One digit is never capped.

Where the room numbers come from: 56 (×1.59) and 1118 (×1.21) were measured in the game. 9 and 182 are worked out the same way in this page, from the width of the number and its € ().

Question 2 · the sticker keythe store channel in planning · the reference belt (planning_effects_h1) has 3 kinds of sticker

Why a smaller font in one row isn't an option: the three stickers alone take px with their badges, plus 24 px of gaps and 12 px between sticker and caption; even with 10 px captions the row needs px, and the channel is 272. Captions cut to one word ("own", "item", "loyalty") still need px.

Why B shrinks the inspection: the slide is 124 px tall. One key row takes 34 px plus a 4 px gap, which leaves 86 px for the brief: SHIFT row 26, gap 8, INSPECTION 16, its name 20, its notice 16 = 86. Two rows leave 52, which is the SHIFT row and one more line. The inspection is still on the top bar's chip.

C needs nothing new to be drawn: the channel already cycles slides (brief, loyalty upgrades, coupons) about every 4 s, with a page dot each. The key slide would come right after the brief, and with today's captions it holds up to 5 kinds in 3 rows. A fourth row doesn't fit the 124 px slide, so longer captions or wider stickers ("+120") would need a rule for the overflow: skip what doesn't fit, as now, or a second key slide.
