Pick, Skip and Cart

The machine's pick, live. You skip the whole draw with the small bin under the tickets. You place a ticket by dropping it in the cart: on an empty slot to add it, on a cart card to swap that card out. Drag, or click to pick up and click again to drop.

Hover SKIP, or pick up a ticket.

What to try

  1. Hover SKIP. The lid opens and all three tickets shake with a shred mark. Click to bin the draw.
  2. Drag a ticket onto a gold slot (+1), or onto a cart card (it turns red with OUT ✕).
  3. Click a ticket without dragging: it sticks to the pointer. Click a slot or a card to drop it.
  4. Change your mind: drop it anywhere outside the cart, or on ◀ BACK. The ticket goes back and SKIP returns.
  5. Cart full: no free slots, so the only drop is a swap. The rule shows without a sign.

What this replaces

  • The display's BIN IT ✕ and the ticket's or bin them all ✕ become the one SKIP bin, shown only before a pick.
  • ADD TO CART and SWAP FOR… go: where you drop the ticket is the choice.
  • The cart view never destroys the draw. Its worst misdrop is putting the ticket back.
  • Register and upgrade prizes (Boosted) don't go to the cart and keep their current pick.
