RECEIPT REWARDS · redraw

The receipt machine redrawn in the art direction the title, the toy machines and the new shift scene (PR #126, #123) share: palette colours only, pixel art on the 640×360 art grid, hard pixel lettering, steel housings with a screw in each corner, lamp light as a dithered pool, and an outline only on what the player touches. It stands at its camera stop to the right of the stage, in front of PR #126's store, under the top bar from the newest shift-scene branch (feature/crowd-shadow-top-bar, v0.84; its store and the machine's stop are unchanged from PR #126). Mock-up only: no rule or flow changes. The visit is the one in plan section 5.4 (v0.61 and later): two keys, three tickets, pick one or skip.

What each part borrows

Neon head sign

"RECEIPT" in teal and "REWARDS" in mustard neon: a pale core, a 1 px tube and a dithered halo, like the title's NEXT CUSTOMER sign. It hangs from two rods like the TOY MACHINES board, in a steel frame with four screws. The neon blinks off for a frame now and then.

title_screen, toy_plate (#118, #121)

Receipt slot and LED

The receipt goes into a steel-lipped slot. The message moves to an amber dot-matrix LED in a steel housing with a screw in each corner, like the COINS and FIGURINES counters. The odds show there as three rarity bars while a key is hovered.

capsule_counter (#121 v0.78)

Stockroom window

The three sealed tickets (the game's own sprites) stand on pedestals behind glass. A lamp in the window lights them with a stepped Bayer pool, the store's lamp light in small. The glass has two reflection stairs.

StoreArt light_at (PR #126), sealed_ticket_*.png

Two chunky keys

NEW ITEMS is cream and UPGRADE is teal: raised caps with a visible depth, which press down when chosen. A ring of indicator lamps blinks round each lit key and holds steady on hover. Unlit keys go steel grey, and grey is never their only sign: the ring is off too.

key_cream, button_teal frames

Prize tray

The tickets ride down into three bays and open there, rarest last, each over a rarity tab (COMMON steel, RARE! teal, LEGENDARY!! gold). A hovered prize lifts 3 px inside a gold frame. "TAKE YOUR PRIZE" becomes "PICK ONE PRIZE".

plan 5.4 visit, rarity_tab

Plinth and SKIP bin

The cabinet stands on the toy machines' steel coin base, widened, with vents and feet. The SKIP pedal bin stands on the floor beside it. Hovering it tips the lid open, shakes the prizes under tomato shred marks, and the LED asks "BIN ALL 3?".

Questions

Q1 · Body colour. Cream enamel (default) stands out against the dark teal store and matches the household washer cabinet. Teal blends into the store; tomato breaks the rule that tomato means a fizzle, CHECKOUT or CLOSED. Try them with the Body buttons.
Q2 · Pixel size at the stop. PR #126 draws the machine at half scale and zooms the camera 2×, so the store behind it shows 4-px pixels while the machine shows 1-px pixels. Default: draw the machine on the 640×360 grid like everything else, and make the stop a pan at zoom 1. Every pixel on screen is then 2 px, as on the title and the toy screen. The machine is a foreground fixture in front of the counter, about 7 items tall.
Q3 · Odds. Default: the hovered key's odds show on the LED as three bars, and the exact odds card (MachineOdds) opens on a click or a long hover, as it does now. The alternative is a cream price card hanging on two chains beside the machine, like the toy screen's lineup.
Q4 · PREMIUM dressing. Default: the same cabinet with a velvet-black sign, gold neon "PREMIUM REWARDS", chasing bulbs, a gold trim, a gold receipt stub, gold indicator rings and RARE-or-better tickets in the window. Toggle PREMIUM to see it.

Real and approximated

  • Real: the palette (art/src/palette.txt); the store behind the machine, ported from PR #126's StoreArt (sky bands, roofs, mullions, shelving, Bayer-dithered lamp pools); the sealed ticket, item and icon sprites; Pixelify for words, rasterised hard at 11 px as UiKit.pixel_label does.
  • Approximated: the digits and the 3×5 small print are hand-drawn bitmap faces standing in for the number font and the body font. The machine is painted in code for the mock-up. In the game it would be art/src/machine/*.txt sources built by tools/pixel_art.py, with the lit and dim states made by @derive tone=dim|off.
  • The prizes shown are examples (Strawberries, a BIG Croissant, Lemon; on PREMIUM a BIG Grapefruit, a BIG Fruit salad, a GOLDEN Banana), and the odds are level 0's NEW ITEMS (65/28/7) and UPGRADE (75/22/3).
