Round 2: what changed

NEW ITEMS and UPGRADES are now split cleanly. NEW ITEMS · ADD TO CART gives you one more item. It always arrives plain. Its rarity is the product's own: basic products are COMMON, stronger products RARE (Milk and Eggs among them), the strongest LEGENDARY. UPGRADES · IMPROVE ONE YOU OWN makes an item you already have better, and is the only way to get BIG or GOLDEN: COMMON is one step on one copy, RARE is two steps on one plain copy (straight to GOLDEN), LEGENDARY is a SHELF UPGRADE (every copy of one item goes one step up; only for an item with 2 or more copies that can step). Golden twin is gone. UPGRADES still opens only after shifts 3, 5 and 7.

The landing shows the difference. Both open your cart on that item. NEW ITEMS: a new plain item flies into an empty slot, NEW IN CART, the count goes up by 1. UPGRADES: an up-arrow flies to the copy you own, it glints and changes in place, UPGRADED, the count stays the same. Try shift 3 with NEW ITEMS, then UPGRADES.

The odds card is exact

The card and the real roll come from one draw process (flow/draw77.js): the card lists every possible draw with its probability; the real roll runs the process forward. flow/check_odds.js compares the two over 100,000 seeded rolls per state (last run: 520 states, 100,000 rolls each, largest gap 0.55 points (BONUS s3 coupons>multipack+ then coupons · REGULAR 2, coupon:final_markdown:0); that is 3.7 standard errors, sampling noise over the printed numbers; 472 of them are BONUS TICKET states, largest gap 0.55 points). Tier % is per drop. A prize's % is its chance to be among the 3.

The rules. No prize twice in one draw. REGISTER never repeats a type. A rarity with nothing in it steps down a tier, then up. NEW ITEMS always holds 1 generally useful item: if none drops, the last drop whose rarity has one is swapped for one of the same rarity (skipped only if all 3 drops roll LEGENDARY and no generally useful LEGENDARY is stocked). The first COUPONS visit holds Breakfast sticker, Multipack or 2 for 1 the same way. REGULAR 3 makes drop 1 RARE or better. UPGRADES only offers an item when one of your copies can take the steps.

STOCK changes what NEW ITEMS can draw: the fresh stock (20 products: 12 COMMON, 6 RARE, 2 LEGENDARY), or a full-collection run with two aisles listed (no new arrivals). Cold cases + Snacks has no LEGENDARY, so its LEGENDARY rolls step down to RARE, and the card says so.

Placeholders: the product rarities (product_rarity.json, until the build measures them), the odds per REGULAR level, and the BIG and GOLDEN numbers. Names and rule texts are the game's own (data/). The run history is invented: after shift 1 NEW ITEMS (Milk), 2 COUPONS (binned), 3 UPGRADES (one Eggs to BIG), 4 REGISTER, 5 UPGRADES (one Milk to GOLDEN), 6 COUPONS (binned).
