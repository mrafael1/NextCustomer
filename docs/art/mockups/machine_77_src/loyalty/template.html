What you are looking at

Left: the RECEIPT REWARDS machine's four chutes (prize types) with a REGULAR meter under each, in three variants (A punch ticket, B stamp strip, C bulb rail; top buttons). Click a chute to "pick" it as the run's next visit: its meter rises, and the odds card animates from the old to the new odds of that chute's next draw. UPGRADES only opens after shifts 3, 5 and 7; a REGISTER pick also stamps the LOYALTY card (REG), and at 3 stamps REGISTER reads CARD FULL. With STAMP DAY on (option B, the default; ?sday=0 turns it off), the visits after shifts 2, 4 and 6 top the card up to 1, 2 and 3 stamps with a free register (DAY) that does not raise the REGISTER meter; switch it off for option A. "new run" resets every meter. The level table is the data the odds come from. Right: the balance sketch, all numbers measured with the game's simulator (greedy drafting, sensible rows; method and caveats in machine_77_src/BALANCE.md). Hover a bar for its runs and confidence.
