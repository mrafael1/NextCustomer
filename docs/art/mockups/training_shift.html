A short, replayable training shift

You get a fixed basket of Eggs and two Bananas and a 10€ target. You arrange the row, scan a Banana to see what it will pay, and check out. Only one order reaches the target, so the one combo teaches placement, scanning and the receipt in one go. It takes under a minute, saves nothing to the run, and can be replayed from the title menu.

The numbers come from today's card data (data/cards/eggs.tres, banana.tres; plan v0.46's Banana chain): Eggs, Banana, Banana = 1 + 4 + 6 = 11€. The first Banana gets the Eggs' ×2 (2 → 4), and the second adds the 4€ before it (2 + 4). Every other order pays 7€: Banana, Banana, Eggs = 2 + 4 + 1 with the charge WASTED, and Banana, Eggs, Banana = 2 + 1 + 4 with both Bananas lone. A 10€ target therefore needs the combo, and a wrong order is 3€ short.

Each stage below is live. Click an item, then a belt slot (or drag it). Hold the pointer on a belt item for 0.6 s to scan it, then press CHECKOUT. The strip above each stage jumps to any state, ▶ Autoplay plays the whole flow, and the log beside it shows what telemetry would write. The art is the game's (a shift-1 capture with the belt and baskets cleared, item sprites from art/items_v2/, sounds from audio/sfx/). The notes, clipboard, end card and title plates are CSS approximations in the palette.

A

Sticky notes: the manager walks you through it

RECOMMENDED

Guided, one step at a time, but never locked. One sticky note on the back wall says what to do next, and a gold ring with a bouncing arrow marks where. The steps are Eggs first, a Banana after it, the second Banana, scan the last Banana, then CHECKOUT. Any action is allowed. A wrong order gets an honest note ("7€: 3€ short") that names the reason from the receipt's own WASTED lines. Time: about 40 s.

B

Clipboard: a checklist, you work it out

Free play with goals. A clipboard on the wall lists four tasks (fill the belt, scan a Banana, reach 10€, CHECKOUT) and ticks them as you go. Nothing points at anything. A HINT button gives three hints in turn, and after 12 s idle the next task blinks. It's the most respectful of players who like puzzles, but a new player can stall at 7€ without knowing why. Time: 45–120 s.

C

Watch, then you: the manager's try, then your turn

A contrast demo first. A ghost hand plays the natural but wrong order (Banana, Banana, Eggs = 7€, the charge WASTED) and the monitor explains it. The items then go back, and the monitor says "Your turn: beat 7€". The monitor is the only trainer, with no notes on the wall. The contrast teaches the "why" well, but players have to watch before they can act, and the demo is skippable. Time: about 60 s.

Recommendation: A, with C's "why" built into its wrong-order note

Why A. The goal is one minute that a first-time player can't get stuck in. A's note always says what to do next and where, and nothing is locked. The player still makes every move, so the combo feels like their own. The scan becomes a required step at the moment it pays off: the player reads "6€ = 2€ + the 4€ before it" on the monitor just before ringing it up.

What A takes from B and C. C's contrast arrives in A only if the player makes the mistake: a wrong order gets the 7€ note with its reason, which is C's lesson without a forced demo. From B, A takes the three-step pips in the top bar (ARRANGE · SCAN · CHECKOUT), so progress shows without a second panel.

Why not B or C alone. B is lovely for players who already know roguelikes, but the 7€ dead end has no explanation unless you ask for a hint, and the playtest (fb-p2) showed that players misread totals. C makes everyone watch 10 s before touching anything, and on a replay that becomes a chore.

Questions (my default in bold)

  1. Which direction? A, sticky notes, with C's reason built into the wrong-order note.
  2. Can you check out below the target? Yes: the count-up plays, the receipt reads "3€ SHORT", and TRY AGAIN appears, with no penalty. The alternative is a CHECKOUT that stays dim until 10€.
  3. Where is it offered? A TRAINING plate on the title, plus a one-time "First shift?" prompt on the first START RUN. No forced tutorial.
  4. Keep the basket at exactly Eggs, Banana, Banana with a 10€ target, or add a decoy (Bread) to make the choice less obvious? Keep 3 items. It's shorter and the combo is the whole lesson.
