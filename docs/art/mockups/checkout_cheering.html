What to try

  1. One big item: at best €90 only the Croissant (€34) is big, so one standee pops. Switch Q1 to quota-share: at €80 the bar is €12 and every item cheers.
  2. Three bigs + a WASTED: watch the crowd grow, the WASTED coupon slump the newest standee, then compare Strict.
  3. Huge item: two pop 0.08 s apart, then the next huge fills the row; the ding freezes everything for 0.85 s, then all hop once.
  4. Quota missed: the verdict sinks the crowd.
  5. Turn on Show keep-clear zones: the green crowd zones never touch the red ones.
  6. Watch the end: they fold down before the printer feed, and the 2x glide to the reward machine shows the gap empty.

Recommendation

  • Best-relative threshold: the cheer means "good for you, this run", and it does not fire on every item in shift 1.
  • Right gap only: the fans in gap A sit close to the receipt and the cashier, and the eye already goes left for the receipt.
  • Soft streak: a WASTED item costs one standee, which reads as a small "aww" rather than a punishment.
  • Cardboard thunks; the count-up's ladder and the ding already carry the joy, a "yay" on top crowds the mix.
