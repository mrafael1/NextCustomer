Receipt machine prizes and loyalty perks · issue #108

You asked to rework the prizes' effects so they're more interesting and satisfying, and to make bonuses more consistent and strategically interesting. The machine's look stays as it is (#77, premium machine). This page is about what you win. Section 1 shows today's prizes on one screen. Section 2 offers three directions for the loyalty perks, drawn as real loyalty tickets and scored on five example rows: click a ticket to put it on your card. Section 3 asks how strong a stack may get. Section 4 covers making a win feel like a win. Section 5 has the questions.

The short version: players almost never choose LOYALTY. On fb-p3, all 19 BOOSTED visits went to UPGRADES and none to LOYALTY. Every perk came from the free STAMP DAY. Of the six perks, two never make a receipt step (Extra redraw, Big basket), the Coupon engine fizzles with 6 of the 8 coupons, and the Category engine is one lump that fits every deck. Only the Slot engine works, and it's the rarest. So the perks need reworking, and so does the choice.

Four questions (details and options in section 5)

  1. Q1 · How strong may a stack get? (section 3) No ceiling (Balatro style), about 1.5× the quota with "perks change the payout, not the price", or a hard cap? Recommended: about 1.5× the quota.
  2. Q2 · Direction for the loyalty perks (section 2): A family loyalty with jackpots, B position and sequence engines, or C build-around jackpots alone? Recommended: A, a hybrid that takes B's Coupon engine and C's Bulk deal, Banana stand and Lemon squeezer.
  3. Q3 · Extra redraw and Big basket: keep them and credit what they earned on the receipt, rework them with new bonuses, or cut them? Recommended: keep and credit them.
  4. Q4 · Why pick LOYALTY when STAMP DAY fills the card for free? Lower STAMP DAY's floor, let a LOYALTY pick level up a perk, or give both a perk and an upgrade? Recommended: lower STAMP DAY's floor.

The choice

Why LOYALTY loses. STAMP DAY (after shifts 2, 4 and 6) tops the card up to 1, 2 and 3 perks for free. So picking LOYALTY on a BOOSTED visit only gets a perk one shift earlier, and costs an item upgrade. The card never holds more than 3 perks.
Why the perks feel flat. Extra redraw and Big basket never make a receipt step, so they never "fire". The Coupon engine pays nothing with 6 of the 8 coupons. The Category engine fits about 97% of dealt decks, so mixing is the default, not a choice. Its pay is one lump on the last product. The Slot engine is the strongest perk (6 of 6 picked, +20 points of win rate) but the rarest (9% on the first draw), and it's about positions, not families. The COMMON tier has only 2 perks, and neither fires.

2Three directions for the loyalty perksQ2

Each direction is a pool of perks with COMMON, RARE and LEGENDARY tiers, drawn as the machine's loyalty tickets. A perk's ticket takes the colour and glyph of the family it boosts, as item tickets do since #105. Perks that name no family (positions, copies) are silver, so they no longer read as Breakfast's gold. Click tickets to put up to 3 on your loyalty card, pick a row, and the belt and receipt rescore. The table at the bottom shows what each perk adds to each row on its own.

3The ceiling: how strong may a stack get?Q1

Family perks stack on purpose, and two things make them stack hard. Regulars grows with the square of your family count (+1, +2, +3 ...), and today a perk's flat bonus goes into the price that the Banana chain passes on (_scan sets prices[slot] after the upgrade bonuses), so every link carries it again. The table scores the same stacks both ways, against shift 5's quota (€73) and shift 8's (€120). "Payout only" is a one-line change: set prices[slot] before _upgrade_flat_bonuses. A chain's first link still passes its full payout, as it does today.
