Price tickets · concept 3 of the summary round · mock-up

Type first: a ticket with no icons

Direction B (colour card), made taller: 108x76 screen px, 54x38 art px at 2x. The family glyphs come off the face. The family is told by colour and a named strip, the price by big hard-pixel digits, and the effect by words. The glyphs come back only in the hover tip.

How each part answers one question

  • Face colour (family, what it works with): the whole card stays in the #105 ticket colour, so a row of mustard reads as "Breakfast combo" before anything else is read.
  • Name strip at the foot (family, what it is): the item's own families as words in hard 3x5 caps on their colours: one family is spelled out (BREAKFAST), two or three use codes (BFST · DAI). It replaces the 9x9 glyph row, so the only pictures near the belt are the groceries.
  • Price window (price): one fixed window in the top-right corner, with 6x8 hard-pixel digits and the € after the number (128€). Its fill shows the state (paper base, pale gold boosted, red 0€).
  • Was-price (price change): the old price sits straight on the face to the left of the window, struck in red, in a 4x7 face whose € has its curve and two bars, so "4€" can't read as "4E". It has no box, so it never looks like a second price.
  • Cream plate (effect): the rule in the game's Atkinson 800 13 px, two lines at most. A target family is a highlighted word in that family's colour (Breakfast), and a keyword is a pixel label tape (BATCH), so the two never look alike.

The 2-second test: 1st, the price (biggest and highest-contrast, in the same corner on every ticket). 2nd, the colour, taken in without reading (the face, then the highlighted word that names it). 3rd, the rule's first line. The name strip is the 4th look, there to confirm, and the colour-blind player's route.

Telling the families apart without colour

  • What it is: the strip at the foot spells it out (BREAKFAST, or the codes BFST · DAI · BAK). It is text, so it doesn't rely on the colour.
  • What it works with: the face colour is always spelled out by the highlighted word in the rule ("+1 per earlier Breakfast"). A ticket with no rule has the colour of its own family, which is the strip's first name.
  • The tip: the glyph and full name of each family, plus the colour's meaning line from the game (FamilyPalette.colour_tip).
  • So nothing is carried by colour alone, and the strip needs no hatch pattern. If playtests still mix up two codes (BAK and BFST are the closest), the fix is a 1-art pattern on the strip's light row: dots for Dairy, dashes for Bakery, solid for Breakfast.

Codes: Produce VEG · Dairy DAI · Bakery BAK · Breakfast BFST · Household HOME · Clearance CLR · Snack SNK · Food FOOD (Food shows only when an item has no other family). One family is spelled in full, because every name fits in 50 art at 3x5.

Weak points, honestly

  • Two families, two places: the face is the "works with" colour and the strip is "what it is". Milk is mustard with a teal DAI segment. That is the #105 rule made visible, but a new player may still ask why Milk isn't teal. The tip explains it; the ticket alone doesn't.
  • Codes are a learning cost: BFST, DAI and BAK must be learned, and BAK/BFST are close at 3x5. Full names only fit with one family.
  • The strip is quiet by design: 5-px-tall caps at 1:1 are readable but small. The strip is a confirmation, not a first read. A player who looks for family first has to rely on colour.
  • Mixed resolutions remain in the plate: the rule is still smooth Atkinson (as in the game) next to hard-pixel digits, chips and strip. It is contained on cream, but it's not pure pixel art.
  • A 2-line rule with a long family word is tight: "Household" or "Clearance" highlighted in 13 px takes about 70 of the 94 px on a line, so a rule naming two families needs the 12 px fallback.
  • The keyword tape is a change to KeywordChip: it reverses the game's outlined chip (ink caps on the calm face) to calm-coloured caps on ink, so the 3x5 caps get a margin inside a 7-art band. If the outlined chip has to stay, the rule bands need 9 art and the plate grows by 2.
  • Taller tickets cost 12 px of the hand row (baskets moved down), the same cost as v2's 76 option.
