A new figurine joins the shelf

Where: the TOY MACHINES screen at the store exit (opened from the results or from the title's REWARDS menu). You spend a coin, a capsule opens and its figurine is shown. Today the figurine then just disappears when you press OK, and you only see it again by opening FIGURINE SHELF. Nothing tells you when an aisle is completed, although completing aisles now unlocks things on the start screen.

This page designs what happens after the capsule opens: the figurine goes to its spot on the shelf, the counts tick, and a completed aisle gets its own moment that says what it unlocked. The coin, crank and capsule animation before it is issue #64. Here a short stand-in plays it.

On a phone, Focus view (on by default under 700 px) shows a 560×480 crop of the stage that follows the action (the machine, then the drawer, then the card), about 2x larger than the whole stage would be. Click or tap the stage to skip, like in the game: a tap speeds up to the next resting point and never skips past a card that needs OK. Use the toolbar to switch style and capsule.

What the game does today

  • One machine per aisle, 30 figurines in all: Cold cases 2, Pantry 2, Household 3, Fruit & veg 8, Bakery 8, Snacks 7 (data/aisles/*.tres, capsule_cards). 1 coin buys 1 capsule (capsule_price).
  • No duplicates, ever. The first capsule from a machine is always its key item. After that, one is drawn at random from the items still left (core/capsule_draw.gd). An empty machine shows SOLD OUT and can't be picked. So this design needs no duplicate step. If duplicates were ever added, that would be a rule change.
  • The reveal (ui/toys/toy_reveal.gd): the figurine at 96×96 on a gold medallion with a sunburst behind it, NEW!, the name, the aisle and "In stock from your next run". It arms after 350 ms. Then OK, a click, Enter or Space closes it.
  • The shelf (ui/toys/figurine_shelf.gd): a separate screen. A wooden cabinet holds one 576×116 section per aisle, in two columns. Each section has its sign, an "n / m" count and its figurines at 48×48, 70 px apart. They are packed from the left in data order, so a new figurine can land in the middle and push the ones after it right. Locked figurines are hidden, and a full aisle gets an ALL COLLECTED tag.
  • The lineup (ui/toys/toy_lineup.gd): when you hover a machine, a price card hangs where the TOY MACHINES board is. It shows the aisle's minis at 2x, in colour once owned and as grey shapes before.
  • Completed aisle = every item of the aisle is collected: its starting products plus all its figurines (StartSetupRules.completed). What that unlocks comes from the start-screen rework (uncommitted, branch feature/start-staff-room, plan 7.2):
    • regular swaps: the run starts with 4 regulars (pack_starter.tres core_cards: Banana ×2, Milk, Eggs). You may swap up to min(completed aisles, regulars) of them (_swap_cap), only for a plain-tier product (the base version, not a golden one) of a completed aisle, and at most 2 swapped regulars from one aisle (staple_swap_aisle_max). So each completed aisle adds 1 swap up to 4, and also adds its products to the choices;
    • at 3 completed aisles: RESTOCK, which swaps one starting basket's pair once a run (restock_min_completed);
    • at 6 completed aisles: choose your aisles (STARTER CART and RUN ONLY) (pair_choice_min_completed). With 6 aisles, 6 completed means the whole collection, 30 / 30.
  • Not built yet: issue #64, the machine's coin, crank and capsule animation (open). It ends on the reveal above. Everything after the reveal on this page is NEW.

Three ways to show the figurine joining the shelf

A

Shelf drawer

RECOMMENDED

After OK, that aisle's real shelf section slides up from the bottom of the screen. The figurine flies from the medallion into its spot, shrinking from 96 to 48, and the figurines after it step aside. The spot lights up, then n / m, the floor plate and FIGURINES tick. The drawer slides away. About 2.1 s after OK.

A completed aisle: the ALL COLLECTED tag slaps on, the figurines hop, and the COMPLETED! card shows what you unlocked.

B

Lineup card

The figurine flies up into the machine's LINEUP card, which already exists on hover. Its grey shape flips into colour. It's the lightest option (1.6 s) and never leaves the machines, but you don't see the shelf: the figurine becomes a mini of 14 art px (28 stage px).

C

Slide to the shelf

The full FIGURINE SHELF screen slides in from the right over the machines. The figurine drops onto its plank, then the shelf screen slides back out to the right. It shows the whole collection every time, but it's the longest (3.3 s) and leaves the machines for every coin.

In this mock-up the shelf screen is a redraw, not a capture: its title plate and FIGURINES chip are hand-drawn approximations of figurine_shelf.gd.

Pick A, the shelf drawer. It shows the figurine landing on the shelf, which is what the shelf is for, without leaving the machines. The coins and the other machines stay in view, so several coins in a row stay quick. It reuses the shelf's own art (cabinet, plank, signs, ALL COLLECTED tag), and the same drawer is the stage for the completed-aisle moment. B is the fallback if A feels slow after many capsules.

The COMPLETED! card NEW

The same card for all three styles. It only shows the lines that apply, and every line is a rule that already exists on the start screen (branch feature/start-staff-room). It needs OK, because it's rare (at most 6 times per profile) and it explains rules. Try "Completes aisle #2", "#3" and "#6" in the toolbar.

Every number on the card comes from data, none is written into the card: 4 and the regulars' names = pack_starter.tres core_cards (Banana ×2, Milk, Eggs); <cap> = _swap_cap = min(completed aisles, regulars); max 2 = staple_swap_aisle_max; 3 = restock_min_completed; 6 = pair_choice_min_completed. The code treats a threshold of 0 as off, so the card hides its RESTOCK line (and its "Next at" mention) when restock_min_completed is 0, and the same for CHOOSE YOUR AISLES. These values are on the uncommitted start-screen branch and wait for a play test, so they may still change. The swap icon is the aisle's glyph with two swap arrows, not a capsule item (capsule items are not always plain tier).

Footer line: "From your next run", or "After your saved run" when the screen was opened from the title with a run saved. That's the same rule as the reveal's stock line, because a saved run keeps the setup it started with.

Reduced motion: driven by the game's own setting, profile.reduced_motion() (the screen already reads it, toy_machines_screen.gd _reduced_motion); this page's toggle stands in for it and starts from the browser's prefers-reduced-motion. No flight, shake, swing or pops. The 96 px figurine leaves with the medallion at once (no second copy at the medallion), the drawer fades in (0.25 s) with the figurine already in its spot, the counts change in place, and the card and SOLD OUT fade in.
Hover and the LED strip: if the aisle completes while that machine's lineup card is showing (hover), the lineup card stays up behind the shade and updates at the tick. The drawer covers the LED strip (y 586-630), where the hint or, after the last coin, the "NO COINS LEFT…" notice shows. Both show again as soon as the drawer has left.

Data it reads: the drawn card and its aisle (from CapsuleCounter.draw), the profile's unlocked items before the draw (for the packing and n−1), balance.aisles, and StartSetupRules.completed before and after the draw (to decide whether the aisle completed, and how many are complete). For the card: the regulars from pack_starter.tres core_cards, the swap cap from _swap_cap (min of completed aisles and regulars), staple_swap_aisle_max, restock_min_completed and pair_choice_min_completed (0 = off, line hidden). NEW The screen keeps the values from before the draw (FIGURINES LED, plate, window, the machine's EMPTY state; only COINS changes at the draw) until the tick and the Sold out beat, instead of refreshing at the draw. It changes nothing in the save: the draw is already saved before any of this plays, so quitting mid-animation loses nothing. Several capsules in one visit play the full beat each time.

Questions (each has a default)

  1. Which style? A shelf drawer, B lineup card, C slide to the shelf. Default: A.
  2. Should the COMPLETED! card wait for OK? Default: yes. It's rare and it explains new rules. With "no", it would close by itself after 3 s.
  3. Several capsules in one visit: keep the full beat every time, or play it ×1.25 faster from the 3rd capsule of the same visit? Default: full beat every time. A run pays 0–3 coins for shifts passed (coins_by_shifts_passed) plus at most 1 overtime coin, coins carry over between visits, and pins cost 12 coins, so a visit usually spends about 1–5 coins: 5 × 2.05 s ≈ 10 s, and a tap already skips the Look beat.
  4. Build it with #64 (as the beat after the capsule opens) or on its own before #64? Default: on its own first. It plays after today's plain reveal, and #64 then slots its coin, crank and capsule in front of it.
