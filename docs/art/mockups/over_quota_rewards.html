Rewarding over quota

Design study for the request "reward over quota (×2, ×3 etc)". Nothing in the game has been changed. All numbers are starting values for the one balance pass at the end of the phase.

1. Recommended: STAR SHIFT recommended

In one line: each shift you pass earns 0 to 3 stars depending on how many times you covered the quota. Stars fill a card that pays coins. Once a week, a 3-star shift turns your next machine visit into PREMIUM.

×1 quota met
Same as today: the lamp shows a check and you pass. No star.
★ first bar
Week 1: ×2. The pip lights during the count, and you hear a xylophone step and a ka-ching.
★★ second bar
Week 1: ×3. A second pip, one step higher.
★★★ top bar (the cap)
Week 1: ×4. Bigger slam, confetti, cheering. Once a week the next CLASSIC visit becomes PREMIUM.
  • The bars rise each week (×2/3/4, ×2/3/5, ×3/4/6, ×3/5/8) because decks get stronger. A shift gives 3 stars at most, however far past the quota it goes.
  • The star card: 18 stars pay 1 coin at the end of the run, 1 coin per run at most. Leftover stars carry over to your next run, so a lost run still counts. This replaces today's overtime coin, which only 9% of runs ever got.
  • When you see it: live during the count-up (the pips light on each ×N stamp), then on the shift receipt ("+47€ OVER TARGET ★★"), then on the final receipt ("STARS 21 → +1 COIN").
  • No stars on a day off, on a week-1 SLIP (a missed quota that is forgiven) or on a failed shift. Shift 16 earns stars, but they only go to the card, because no machine visit comes after it.
  • The PREMIUM upgrade never applies on payday shifts 4/8/12 (already PREMIUM) or on shift 16. It has its own on/off switch in the data.

Why this one

  • ×2 is not special. It happens on 54% of all shifts, so a reward at plain ×2 would feel automatic. The stars are graded and capped.
  • The top end grows fast. Late shifts reach ×10 or more for the top 10% of runs, so a reward that grows with each lap (×5 = 5 rewards) would explode. A cap of 3 stars and rising bars keep it fair.
  • Coins don't change who wins, so the star card needs no quota refit. Only the weekly PREMIUM upgrade adds in-run power, and that is why it gets an on/off switch and its own balance check.
  • It fixes a playtest problem: players thought a lower total wins. The ×N stamp that already exists now visibly pays something.

3. Alternatives considered (ranked)

  1. Overtime upgrade alone (beat the week's lap bar → next CLASSIC visit becomes PREMIUM, once a week). Strong goal, but on its own it rewards only the very top shifts and needs a quota refit. It is in the package as the 3-star bonus.
  2. Tip card (laps punch a card that turns into coins and carries over). Almost the same as stars. Its carry-over idea is in the package, but stars read better than punches next to the ×N lamp.
  3. Bonus envelope (the week's stars fill an envelope; a full one gives +1 odds on the Payday tickets). Bounded and safe, but the payoff comes at the review, far from the count, so the moment is weak. Good swap if you'd rather have no PREMIUM upgrade.
  4. Tip jar (bank tokens and spend them at the machine for +1 odds or a reroll). The most strategic option, but costly: a new save format, a simulator spending policy, machine UI, and reroll replay on resume. It also overlaps the machine's ADD/SWAP offer.
  5. Lucky lap (×3 gives the next visit +1 odds, ×5 gives +2). It pays on every shift, and ×3 happens on 33% of shifts, mostly in strong runs. That feeds the snowball.
  6. Hot streak odds (the same idea, one visit only). It does nothing from shift 13 on, where the odds are already at the top, so it is invisible exactly where the big laps happen.
  7. Stretch goal (call your target lap before the deal; hit it for a better visit, miss it for a worse one). Real decisions, but the most expensive option. The penalties work against "more is better", and it depends on how exact the planning projection is, which hasn't been checked.
  8. Carry or Cash (after a ×2 shift, choose a lower next quota or a coin). A lower quota as a prize risks the "lower total wins" mix-up. It also adds a prompt on about half the shifts and bends the quota curve.

The simulator already plays to maximise its total, so it needs no new strategy. It only needs a stars field in its records. Making the margin give machine power goes against the plan's current default ("not in 1.0", section 12), so that part needs your OK and a changelog entry.

5. Questions

Q1. Can going over quota give machine power? This is the weekly 3-star CLASSIC → PREMIUM upgrade. The plan currently says no for 1.0.
Default: yes, behind its on/off switch, measured in the end balance pass. The other option is coins and receipt only.
Q2. Flat bars (×2/×3/×5 every week) or rising bars (×2/3/4 … ×3/5/8)?
Default: rising, because late shifts reach ×10 or more and would otherwise take almost all the stars.
Q3. Should stars replace the overtime coin, with leftover stars carried to the next run?
Default: yes to both.
Q4. How does this mock-up feel? Is the 3-star slam big enough, and is the PREMIUM band clear? Should the star card sit on screen during the shift or only on the receipt?
Default: as shown, with the card visible during the count.
