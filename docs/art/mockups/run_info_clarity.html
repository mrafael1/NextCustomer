Run info: where am I, what's next, what do I own

This page is about the top bar of the shift screen and a run info view that opens from it. Today a new player can't easily tell how far into the run they are, what today's rules are, what comes next, or what they own. Every stage below uses a real capture of the four-weeks build at shift 13 of 16 as its backdrop. Only the top bar and the panel it opens are redrawn, with the game's chip frames, icons, palette and fonts.

All three options (A, B and C) are live. Pick a run moment (shift 3, 8 or 13). Pass today lights today's pip, and Miss today shows a slip, or the end of the run. Tap the bar to open the panel. Anything the game doesn't have yet is marked NEW.

The three options

A Clearer bar only

The week chip and the day number merge into one run track: "13/16", then 16 pips in 4 week groups, each underlined in its week's colour (the time card's colours). Played days show ✓ or ✗, today is gold, and the coming reviews are mustard NEW. The day chip becomes a two-line TODAY chip: today's event with its name, then the week's effect. LOYALTY keeps its stamps but loses the word. Nothing else changes: the track still reopens the time card, and the full list stays under Cart › Run info.

Good: answers "where am I" and "today's rules" at a glance, with no new screen. Weak: "what's next" and "what do I own" stay scattered.

B Today's bar + a full run page

The bar stays as it is. ☰ gets a Run info entry NEW that opens a large page in the title's popup frame (860 wide): the whole run as 16 cells, today, what's next, the stakes, what you own and the coins. Cart's Run info tab moves here.

Good: everything in one place. Weak: it's two clicks away, it's wider and covers the back of the belt, and the bar's own problems stay: the event's name is still cut first, and the run's length still isn't on the bar.

C RECOMMENDED A's bar + a run slip that drops from the track

A's bar. Tapping the track drops a run slip NEW under it, the way today's day slip drops. It is the time card's mustard card with paper inside, 600 wide and about 280 tall. It shows the 16-shift track with its PAYDAY and WIN tags, then two columns. TODAY lists every rule in force, where each one hits the belt, and the stakes. NEXT shows tomorrow and the next review. Under them, one line of named stamps, the worn pin and the cart, then the coins so far. It ends above the belt, so the belt, the slots and the baskets stay in sight. The time card still plays at the start of each week. ☰ › Run info opens the same slip.

Recommendation: C

  • One glance, one tap. Without a tap, the bar answers "where am I" (13/16 and the pips) and "what are today's rules" (both lines, with the event's name). One tap on the track answers the rest. The slip covers the monitor but ends by y 347, above the belt's rail (y 350), so you can still read the row you are planning.
  • It folds four places into one. The time card's reopen, the day slip, Cart's Run info tab and the hover-only stamp names all become the slip, and Cart goes back to being the cart.
  • It is built from parts the game already has: the week colours and pip glyphs (WeekChip), the day slip's drop (DaySlip), the time card's card and paper (WeekCard), the stamp rings (LoyaltyCard.make_ring), the pin faces (PinFace) and the "where" lines (DaySlip.where_lines).
  • It gives the event's name room. Today the day chip is what TopBar.fit_inspection cuts first, so on a busy row the name never shows. In C the TODAY chip takes all the room left over, about 270 px, and its first line drops back to the effects only when the full line doesn't fit (see the spec).

Why a new short_text and not notice_text: line 2 has about 170 px after its key. The weeks' notice_text is 31 to 44 characters ("Miss a quota? It is a slip: the run goes on." is about 290 px at 13 px). receipt_text ("Clearance +2€") exists only for Bakery and Clearance, and it leaves out the quota. So each week gets one short line: "misses are slips (2 allowed)", "Bakery +2€ · quota +10%", "quota +15% · odds +1", "Clearance +2€ · quota +15%". The slip's week line uses it too, and the hovers keep notice_text.

Questions (each has a default)

  1. Track tap: open the run slip instead of reopening the time card? Default: yes. The slip holds the week's days too, and the time card still plays at the start of each week.
  2. Bar words: drop "LOYALTY" (stamps only, named in the slip and on hover), and keep "Cart"? Default: drop LOYALTY, keep "Cart 13/15". Cart is the only way into the deck view. Dropping LOYALTY frees the room that the TODAY chip needs.
  3. Where does a Day off pin's SKIP tag go? Default: it takes the place of the "TODAY" key on line 1 while today can be taken off ("SKIP ›", teal, 12 px bold, 2 px border; "UNDO ‹" when armed). It is about as wide as the key, so the width budget doesn't change. The bonus pill needs no place: bonus_after_inspection is off in this build, and paydays are told by the monitor's PAYDAY announce. If the pill comes back, it goes in the slip's NEXT box.
  4. Show "if you stopped now: N coins" in the slip, and say what coins buy? Default: yes. Today coins only appear on the final receipt, so a player doesn't know that 8 passed shifts are worth a coin, or that 12 coins buy a locked pin.

What this is based on

  • ui/top_bar.gd (NC-weeks): the row is x 348, y 9, 916×38 with gaps of 12: SHIFT n/m (hidden with weeks), week chip, day chip, LOYALTY, then Cart and ☰. ☰ holds only Export log. FIT_MARGIN 8. fit_inspection fits the day chip first. InspectionTag.fit_width drops the pill's "PASS ›" and shortens the pill, then cuts the text to the effects only ("Fruit & veg +1€"), then adds an ellipsis. Only if the chip is still cut does the week chip lose its name, then its pips.
  • ui/inspection_tag.gd: "D13", then "Inspection · −1 slot" (CAPTION), "Review · …" (REVIEW_CAPTION) or "Today: Fresh delivery (Fruit & veg +1€)" (TODAY_TEXT, BOON_TEXT), or "Normal day". Tints from tint_of: green light, tomato pale, mustard light, grey light. A tap drops DaySlip (300 px, a 0.32 s drop). It also holds the bonus pill and the Day off SKIP tag.
  • ui/week_chip.gd: "W4", the short name, 9 px pips (PAID green light + ink check, SLIP tomato + paper cross, NOW gold with an ink ring, TODO paper at 30%), flash() 0.8 s from 3×. A tap reopens the time card.
  • ui/week_card.gd: the time card is a MUSTARD_PALE card around a PAPER table ("WEEK 4 OF 4", a row per day, MANAGER'S REVIEW, "REVIEW PASSED · 2 PREMIUM TICKETS" or "PASS = RUN WON"; the training week's "THIS WEEK ALLOWS 2 MISSES").
  • ui/premium_announce.gd + presentation/payday.gd: on review shifts before a payday, the monitor prints "PASS THE REVIEW · PAYDAY · 2 PREMIUM TICKETS", then hangs "PAYDAY NEXT" from the bar's foot. After a pass, the receipt prints "INSPECTION NEXT SHIFT: …", "MANAGER'S REVIEW NEXT SHIFT: …" or "TOMORROW: …".
  • ui/deck_view.gd: the "Run info" tab inside Cart: UPGRADES, INSPECTIONS (this shift and next, as text) and STOCK.
  • NC-weeks data: 16 shifts in 4 weeks (week_length 4). Weeks: Training, Bakery festival, Holiday rush, Clearance week. Inspections on 3, 7, 11 and 15, reviews on 4, 8, 12 and 16, boon days on 2, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13 and 14, paydays after the reviews on 4, 8 and 12. Quotas 30 to 149. Up to 2 slips on shifts 1 to 4. Coins by shifts passed: 0 until 8, 1 from 8, 2 for a win, plus at most 1 overtime coin (one per 8 567€ over quota). Coins buy capsules (1) or a locked pin (12). Upgrades come from the machine's UPGRADE choice (loyalty_per_draw 1, register_cap 3).
  • Pins: RunState.pins, shown on the lanyard by the monitor (ui/badge/pin_lanyard.gd), drawn by ui/badge/pin_face.gd. Recycle: "RE", face_colour 5 (green), "Redrawn items go back in the pile; +1 redraw a shift".
  • The events on this page follow the real schedule. Which boon or malus lands on which day is only an example, because the game draws them.
