4 · Tickets: same size, a finished surface

The ticket stays 54×32 art px (108×64) in every direction; v1's taller ticket is gone. What reads as unfinished is the finish, not the size. All three directions apply these rules:

  • Every number is drawn from one hard-pixel digit sheet: big 4×7 (the "1" is 3 wide) and mini 3×5, at 2x.
  • Rule words stay Atkinson, always on paper or cream.
  • The price sits in one fixed window that never overhangs in planning.
  • Glyphs get an ink outline only (no paper halo).
  • Corners are die-cut, the edge is a single art px of ink, and the ticket casts the same (1,1) shadow as everything else.

On the 0€ slot, the ticket owns the amount and the talker owns the cause.

Each 3x zoom holds four tiles: the GOLDEN stress ticket (128€, 3 glyphs, 2-line rule) in planning and at its count beat (looping), the Spot check slot with a card, and the same slot empty.
