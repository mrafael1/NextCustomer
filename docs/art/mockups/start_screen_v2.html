Start screen v2: pins, completed aisles, staple swaps and today's stock

Layout A of start_screen.html (the clock-in board) with the four requests decided on 2026-11-03: (1) click the badge or a worn pin to change pins before launching; (2) every TODAY'S STOCK sign shows its aisle's completion (5/7, 8/8 ★); (3) each completed aisle opens one staple swap: click a staple and pick a base item from a completed aisle; (4) today's stock is 3 aisles drawn from your unlocked ones, 6 products are dealt from 2 of them and the 3rd is NOT DEALT. Completing 3 aisles opens RESTOCK (swap one dealt aisle), and completing all 6 lets you choose both dealt aisles.

Try it: pick a PROFILE (new save, 1, 3 or 6 completed aisles). Click a staple that has a gold SWAP › chip to open the picker, then pick an item and watch the cart change. Click a swapped staple to change it back. Use RESTOCK › (from 3 completed) to swap a dealt aisle; the new 6 drop into the deal. Click the badge to change pins: the EXAMPLE pin Coupon book shows how a pin that brings cards fills the cart. Hover tickets, aisles and pins for their rules. Esc closes a panel, 1 to 3 pick a pack, Enter opens the store. Gold PROPOSAL tags below mark my proposals. Green DECIDED tags mark your decisions.

Rules spec

RuleWhat it saysStatus
Completed aisleAn aisle is complete when every item of it is collected. For the base aisles that means their capsules too: Cold cases 5 base + Cream, Ham ends (7); Pantry 5 + Sugar cubes, Mixed herbs (7); Household 4 + Egg timer, Chipped mug, Odd socks (7). Machine aisles: Fruit & veg 8, Bakery 8, Snacks 7. Derived from ProfileState.unlocked_items, never saved separately. Shown as n/m on each stock sign, with ★ when complete.DECIDED
Tiers don't countCompletion counts products, not their BIG and GOLDEN tiers. Tiers are never stocked or collected.PROPOSAL
Unlocked aisleA base aisle is always unlocked. A machine aisle is unlocked once it holds aisle_listable_min (4) items, today's "listable" rule. Before that its items still reach runs as new arrivals (the end-cap).PROPOSAL
Today's stockEach run draws 3 aisles from your unlocked aisles. The 6 shared products are dealt from 2 of them. The 3rd stays in the run's stock but is not dealt from.DECIDED
How the 3 are drawnA partial Fisher-Yates over the unlocked aisles in data order, on the run seed's derived stream stock_aisles (it replaces surprise_list). The first 2 drawn are dealt from, the 3rd is NOT DEALT. Same seed and profile, same stock. It is saved in the run, as today's stock is.PROPOSAL
The NOT DEALT aisleIts items stay in the run's stock, so the RECEIPT REWARDS machine's NEW ITEMS can still give them. They are only left out of the starting deal. Unlocked aisles that weren't drawn read NOT TODAY and give nothing this run (apart from new arrivals).PROPOSAL (my reading of "stays locked")
Staples shelf in the dealThe deal's pool is the 6 staples (Banana, Bread, Milk, Eggs, Coffee, Soup) plus the 2 dealt aisles' collected items, as today. This keeps the generally_useful guarantee and the data test "the staples alone fill a deal".PROPOSAL (Q2)
Capsule products dealtA machine aisle can only deal if capsule products can be dealt, so random_capsules goes on, with only the items you own (Fruit & veg at 4/8 deals those 4). Today it is off (TODO(S2)), and with it off a machine aisle would deal nothing.PROPOSAL (Q3)
New save3 unlocked aisles means all 3 are stock, and the seed picks which 2 are dealt from. The alternative is to fix run 1 to Cold cases + Pantry (food only, no Household) as a tutorial deal.PROPOSAL (Q4)
Staple swapsEach completed aisle opens one swap, capped at the 4 staples: with N completed, up to min(N, 4) staples are swapped. A swap replaces one staple with any base item of a completed aisle. The choice is saved to the profile for the next runs and can be changed on this screen.DECIDED
Swaps aren't tied to aislesSwaps are a count, not slots tied to particular aisles. Any open swap can take an item from any completed aisle.PROPOSAL
Swap pool"Base item" read as the plain tier of any collected product of a completed aisle, capsule products included. Read literally as "base_cards only", completing Fruit & veg, Bakery or Snacks would open a swap with nothing new to pick (they have no base cards). The SWAP POOL toggle shows both.PROPOSAL (Q1)
copy_max across the cartA swapped-in item counts with the other staples, today's deal and the most copies any offered pack holds (copies_for_offer's rule) and stays at 2 or fewer. The picker greys it out with "MAX 2 IN CART". The Banana bunch exception is unchanged.PROPOSAL
Plain tier onlyNo BIG or GOLDEN tiers and no coupons. A staple is a plain product, and UPGRADES can still grow it in the run.PROPOSAL
Max 2 from one aisleAt most 2 swapped staples from the same aisle, so a full set of swaps doesn't turn the staples into a single aisle's set. This is the "maybe with some rules" for 4 swaps.PROPOSAL
Swaps don't re-dealThe deal is made with the saved staples (they count toward its copies). Changing a staple on this screen keeps today's deal and packs. It can't re-deal, so it is never a reroll. The fair-deal check is not run again: the player chose the change.PROPOSAL (Q5)
Losing completionIf an aisle stops being complete (the debug panel's reset collection), its swaps fall back to the original staples, last swap first.PROPOSAL
RESTOCK (3–5 ★)With 3 completed aisles you can swap one stock aisle for another unlocked aisle. The NOT DEALT aisle stays NOT DEALT.DECIDED
RESTOCK's fairnessOnce a run, before OPEN STORE. You choose the aisle to take out and the one to put in, and only then the new 6 are dealt, so you never preview the options. The deal comes from a derived stream starting_deck_restock of the run seed with the new pair: deterministic, so the same choice gives the same 6. The packs stay. The run is saved right after it (run save start_restock), so quitting neither rerolls it nor gives the token back. You may switch between the first deal and the restocked one (both are known), and that's all.PROPOSAL
Fair-deal check after RESTOCKThe check runs on the new 6 as it does today, but may only re-deal the 6 (up to fair_deal_redeals on the restock stream). It never swaps a pack, so the packs on screen never change under the player.PROPOSAL
Choose the pair (6 ★)With 6 completed aisles you choose the 2 aisles dealt from.DECIDED
How the pair is chosenThe pair is a profile preference (stock_pair), applied when the next run is dealt, so its deal is never shopped. On this screen, changing it uses the same once-a-run token as RESTOCK and is saved for the next runs. The 3rd aisle is the seed's: the first in the seed's aisle order that isn't in the pair.PROPOSAL
Pins on this screenClick the badge, a worn pin or the pin text to open BADGE PINS over the board. It lists your owned pins and pin_slots (1 now, 2 later), saves to the profile and re-wears the waiting run (RunPins.wear, then the run save). A pin's starting cards show live in FROM YOUR PINS and count toward 15.DECIDED
Pins and the dealA pin change never re-deals. The fair-deal check still ignores pins, as v1 Q2 left it.PROPOSAL
Cart over 15If a pin's cards would take the cart past deck_limit (15), WEAR is greyed out with "cart full". No pin brings cards today.PROPOSAL

Open questions for you

  1. Q1 · What is a "base item" of a machine aisle? Fruit & veg, Bakery and Snacks have no base_cards, only capsule items. My proposal: the plain tier of any collected product of the aisle (capsule items included). If you meant "base_cards only", completing a machine aisle opens a swap but adds nothing new to pick from. See the SWAP POOL toggle on the 3 and 6 profiles.
  2. Q2 · Do the 6 staples stay in the deal's pool next to the 2 dealt aisles (today's behaviour, my default)? Or are the 6 dealt from the 2 aisles only? Then a small pair (Household 4 base) could be thin, and the generally_useful guarantee needs another home.
  3. Q3 · Turn random_capsules on? Without it, a dealt machine aisle deals nothing, and the "dealt from 2 aisles" rule only works for the base aisles. That is v1's Q3, now forced.
  4. Q4 · Run 1 on a new save: does the seed pick which 2 of the 3 base aisles are dealt from (my default), or is run 1 fixed to Cold cases + Pantry?
  5. Q5 · Staple swaps and the fair-deal check: a swap made on this screen keeps today's deal and doesn't run the check again (my default). Or should the screen warn when the new cart fails shift 1's check?
  6. Q6 · RESTOCK: once a run, choose blind, then you may switch between the two known deals. Is that the right strength? Or should it be blind with no going back, or allowed every run with no limit?
  7. Q7 · The 5th completed aisle unlocks nothing new (4 swaps is the cap). Should it give something, e.g. a 2nd RESTOCK a run, or should "choose the pair" move to 5?
  8. Q8 · Unlocked machine aisle: at 4 items (my default, today's listable rule), or from its first capsule?
  9. Q9 · The NOT DEALT aisle: I read "stays locked" as "in today's stock for NEW ITEMS prizes, not dealt from". Or should it give nothing at all this run? Then it is only a teaser sign.
  10. Q10 · Naming: "staples" now means two things: the 4 padlocked cards and the 6-product staples shelf. Should the 4 cards be renamed (e.g. "regulars") on this screen?
  11. Defaults I took (say if any is wrong): swap choices persist across runs; KEEP returns a staple to its original; staple tiles are shown one per slot (not grouped); the picker shows every completed aisle's items, greyed with a reason when blocked; the EXAMPLE pin Coupon book is only there to show a pin's cards in the cart, it is not a content proposal.
