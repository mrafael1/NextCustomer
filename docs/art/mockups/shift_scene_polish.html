Shift scene polish

Six changes to the shift screen. The pictures marked today are real captures (debug/screen_capture, shift 5, quota €80, the --effects belt; the count stills are --checkout=6 / 8.5 / 10). The proposals are drawn on the same 1280×720 stage, at the stage's own coordinates, with the game's art PNGs, palette and fonts, so you can see where each one would sit. Each section shows today beside the proposal. Where there's a real choice I give 2 or 3 lettered variants; the recommended one has a mustard border.
