Keywords guide: one page that explains every word on the tickets

The tickets and the monitor use short words: families (Breakfast, Bakery…), keyword chips (CHAIN, BATCH, PAIR, MORNING, WASTED), coupons, tiers (BIG, GOLDEN) and shift words (redraw, Spot check). Today each one is explained only in its hover tooltip (a family's is one line, a keyword's two or three sentences). This page is NEW: a guide you open from the in-run ≡ menu (and the title screen), or straight from any tooltip by right-clicking while it shows. Each entry has the rule in one sentence, a worked example with real numbers, a short looping clip made from the game's own sprites and v0.95 tickets (items ride in, then pay out one by one, with the rail strip lighting up from whoever gives a ×), and a real monitor capture at 1×.

The stage below is the game's 1280×720 screen, with a real capture of the shift as its backdrop. Click any entry in the guide to switch. 11 entries are fully built (CHAIN, BATCH, PAIR, MORNING, WASTED, Breakfast, 2 for 1, Multipack, GOLDEN, BIG, Spot check); the others show their rule and example, with the clip marked "in the build". Every number was checked against the scoring rules (docs/PROTOTYPE_PLAN.md sections 3 and 5) and the card files in data/cards/.

The three variants

A

Staff handbook

RECOMMENDED

A cream ring binder over the shaded shift. Group tabs across the top, the index on the left, one entry on the right: rule, clip, worked example, a monitor capture. Right-clicking a tooltip opens it at that entry.

  • + Room for all 37 entries, a clip with tickets at full 2× size and the capture at 1×.
  • + Same page from the menu and from a tooltip: one place to learn.
  • − It covers the whole belt while open.
B

On the monitor

The shop monitor grows into a big screen: the bay shows the key item, the paper side the rule and example (a button swaps in the real capture); the clip plays on a belt under it; a chip strip at the bottom picks the entry.

  • + Feels part of the shop; ties straight to "see it on the monitor".
  • − The chip strip holds one group at a time and gets crowded; the capture and the text can't show together.
C

Pop-up card

A card pops up right above the tooltip you right-clicked, with a small 1× clip; ◀ ▶ steps through its group; ALL opens a compact list.

  • + Fastest: you never leave the belt; the belt stays visible.
  • − The 1× clip has no tickets or frames, only prices; poor as a page to browse from the menu.
Recommended: A, the staff handbook.

It is the only one that fits both jobs, browsing from the menu and a quick look from a tooltip, without shrinking the clip (the clip is the part that teaches: you watch the order, the frames, who gives the ×, and the payout). It reuses pieces players already know: the v0.95 tickets, the chain and batch frames, the rail strips, the stickers (+2, ×2, WASTED) and the monitor's wording. B is a good fallback if you want it more in-world; C's "opens right where you clicked" can be kept as A's deep link, which jumps straight to the entry.
